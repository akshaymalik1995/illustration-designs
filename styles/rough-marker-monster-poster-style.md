Rough Marker Monster Poster - Design Language

A naive children's poster system built from thick black marker outlines, rough crayon-pastel color fills, cream paper grain, oversized goofy creature forms, and chunky hand-lettered black text.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Overall Style Category: Naive rough marker children's poster with monster-like object-character energy.
- Composition And Layout: One oversized flat subject dominates the page, with empty cream paper pockets used for irregular black hand lettering and tiny scribble marks.
- Subject Placement: The subject is cropped large and slightly off balance, usually occupying more than half the frame and pressing toward one or more edges.
- Camera Or Perspective: Flat frontal drawing with no lens behavior and almost no constructed perspective; scale and overlap create the only depth.
- Typography Style: Chunky black hand lettering, uneven and playful, placed around the main shape as part of the drawing.
- Color Palette: Warm cream paper, deep black-brown outline and lettering, one dominant saturated body color, yellow-orange accents, white eyes or teeth, and a few pale detail marks.
- Graphic Elements: Short hatch marks, dots, cheek circles, small rectangles, uneven teeth or eye marks, scribble bursts, and rough accent shapes.
- Texture And Finish: Dry marker or oil-pastel grain, broken fill, edge wobble, paper speckle, and lightly scanned analog surface.
- Lighting: Flat ambient paper lighting with no dramatic shadow, glossy highlight, or realistic material reflection.
- Mood And Reference: Mischievous classroom-poster humor, naive monster drawing, absurd handwritten warning, and handmade storybook directness.

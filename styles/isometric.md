Isometric Illustration Style

Interpret the user's idea and communicate it as a precise, elegant isometric illustration — depth without perspective, systems made visible.

Creative process: find the structure or system inside the idea, then stage it as a small isometric world or diorama the viewer can read at a glance.

Every image must feature:

- True isometric projection: 30° angles, no vanishing points, parallel edges
- Clean vector construction with crisp edges
- Consistent light source; flat side-shading (each face one tone: light / mid / dark)
- A contained diorama feel — floating platform or rounded base
- Small-scale human figures or objects to convey scale when helpful
- Limited harmonious palette (4–6 colors) with soft contrast
- Subtle long shadows cast on the ground plane
- Orderly composition; aligned to the isometric grid

Mood: precise, modern, quietly playful. The aesthetic of premium SaaS landing pages and technical explainers.

Avoid: perspective distortion, painterly texture, clutter, realistic lighting, harsh gradients.

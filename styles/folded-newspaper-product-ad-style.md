Folded Newspaper Product Ad - Design Language

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Category: folded newspaper advertorial with photorealistic product hero
- Composition: a nearly full-bleed newspaper page acts as the background; large headlines, stamp labels, red sidebar tab, and dense body columns create an editorial grid; one oversized product cutout occupies the foreground and covers parts of the page
- Mood: bold, appetizing, editorial, promotional, slightly nostalgic, and tactile, with a Hong Kong broadsheet-ad energy without copying any brand

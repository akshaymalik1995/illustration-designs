Folded Newspaper Product Ad - Design Language

A folded broadsheet product advertisement style built from dense bilingual newspaper typography, worn off-white paper, black and antique-gold display headlines, stamped label graphics, one red editorial sidebar tab, and a huge photorealistic hero product overlapping the page foreground.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Style Category: folded newspaper advertorial with photorealistic product hero
- Composition Logic: a nearly full-bleed newspaper page acts as the background; large headlines, stamp labels, red sidebar tab, and dense body columns create an editorial grid; one oversized product cutout occupies the foreground and covers parts of the page
- Subject Placement: the hero product is placed across the lower half or center, large enough to interrupt body copy and page modules, often cropped by the image edge
- Camera Or Perspective: background page is flat frontal with slight paper buckling; product uses commercial three-quarter close-up photography with shallow depth and rich highlights
- Texture: creased paper, uneven folds, ink rub, newspaper grain, distressed stamp marks, soft scanning, and subtle cast shadows from the foreground object
- Mood: bold, appetizing, editorial, promotional, slightly nostalgic, and tactile, with a Hong Kong broadsheet-ad energy without copying any brand

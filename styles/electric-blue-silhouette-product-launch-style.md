Electric Blue Silhouette Product Launch - Design Language

A sparse premium consumer-tech launch poster style built from black negative space, a centered product silhouette, electric-blue rim lighting, a glowing horizontal platform, soft reflection, giant cropped blue background typography, and clean white announcement copy.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Style Category: minimal dark consumer-electronics launch poster, blue silhouette product teaser, premium social product announcement
- Composition Logic: A single hero object sits in front of a giant cropped blue background word on a black stage. A bright blue horizontal light plane creates the tabletop, reflection, and visual anchor while sparse white text sits below.
- Subject Placement: Main product occupies about 18 to 32 percent of the frame, centered and mostly silhouetted, with reflection extending downward into the blue foreground.
- Camera Perspective: Straight-on low-to-mid product view with slight heroic scale and no busy environmental depth.
- Typography Behavior: Huge background word in blue, cropped by frame edges and partially hidden by the product; clean white geometric sans-serif launch copy below with strong scale contrast.
- Color Palette Behavior: Dominantly black and navy with electric-blue glow and white text. No warm accent palette; the only tiny accent may be a white status light.
- Graphic Elements: Giant cropped word, horizontal blue light plane, soft reflection, tiny status dot, simple lower text stack.
- Texture Finish: Glossy consumer-tech surface treatment, dark plastic or glass, soft bloom, subtle haze, smooth gradient falloff.
- Lighting: Low-key black stage with blue rim light from the floor and sides, bright horizontal cyan-blue edge, soft reflection and deep shadows.
- Mood: Quiet, premium, suspenseful, product-launch teaser with strong cool-light atmosphere.

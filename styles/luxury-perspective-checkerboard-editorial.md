Luxury Perspective Checkerboard Editorial - Design Language

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Composition: A sparse studio-white poster field is interrupted by one dramatic perspective checkerboard plane and one monumental low-angle subject. The subject overlaps an enormous script word that behaves like environmental architecture.
- Graphic elements: Clean checkerboard geometry, rare accent dots or diamonds, and hard-edged perspective surfaces.

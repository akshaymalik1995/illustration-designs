Blue HUD Macro Creator Tech Poster - Design Language

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Category: premium 3D creator-tech poster, macro product ad, HUD-heavy social commerce visual
- Composition: A giant foreground product is clamped or presented by oversized hands, while typography and feature cards wrap around it in stacked layers. The product overlaps almost every graphic layer, giving a dense commercial hierarchy.
- Palette: Mostly deep navy, cyan, ice blue, and white with black shadows. A single gold or amber badge supplies warm contrast. Green or orange indicator lights may appear only as tiny accent points.
- Graphic elements: Rounded HUD panels, radial dials, pill meters, small interface icons, transparent rectangles, diagonal streaks, thin lines, glow boxes, spec labels.
- Lighting: Directional rim lights from upper left and cyan side glows, deep shadows, bright specular highlights, mild lens flare and atmospheric blue haze.
- Mood: Futuristic creator-product launch, technical, premium, feature-dense, dramatic, and tactile.

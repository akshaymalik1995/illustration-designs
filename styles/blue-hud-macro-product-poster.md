Blue HUD Macro Creator Tech Poster - Design Language

A high-density blue creator-tech advertisement style built around macro 3D hardware heroes, oversized ribbed gloves, electric-blue HUD panels, massive condensed typography, glossy glass cards, and one warm gold performance badge.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Style Category: premium 3D creator-tech poster, macro product ad, HUD-heavy social commerce visual
- Composition Logic: A giant foreground product is clamped or presented by oversized hands, while typography and feature cards wrap around it in stacked layers. The product overlaps almost every graphic layer, giving a dense commercial hierarchy.
- Subject Placement: Main product occupies 45 to 65 percent of the image area, usually diagonal or vertical, cropped near the center with foreground fingers cutting into corners.
- Camera Perspective: Close macro perspective with slight wide-angle exaggeration, low-to-mid angle, heroic product scale, shallow depth cues.
- Typography Behavior: Massive condensed sans-serif headlines, all caps, white to icy gray, cropped at edges or occluded by product. Secondary labels are small, compact, and placed inside translucent HUD modules.
- Color Palette Behavior: Mostly deep navy, cyan, ice blue, and white with black shadows. A single gold or amber badge supplies warm contrast. Green or orange indicator lights may appear only as tiny accent points.
- Graphic Elements: Rounded HUD panels, radial dials, pill meters, small interface icons, transparent rectangles, diagonal streaks, thin lines, glow boxes, spec labels.
- Texture Finish: Glossy 3D plastic, soft rubber ribs, glass screen reflections, subtle noise, bloom, haze, and high-spec ad polish.
- Lighting: Directional rim lights from upper left and cyan side glows, deep shadows, bright specular highlights, mild lens flare and atmospheric blue haze.
- Mood: Futuristic creator-product launch, technical, premium, feature-dense, dramatic, and tactile.

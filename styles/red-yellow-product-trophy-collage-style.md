Red Yellow Product Trophy Collage - Design Language

A fast-food billboard inspired product collage system with saturated red and yellow poster blocks, oversized condensed typography, glossy cutout products, and a central world-champion trophy silhouette assembled from representative unbranded product objects.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Overall Style Category: Fast-food inspired product launch poster and high-contrast retail collage.
- Composition And Layout: A strict poster grid with a large top headline, central product-built trophy silhouette, side accent tabs, and a narrow bottom strip.
- Subject Placement: The product sculpture occupies the center and most of the vertical height, with products scaled for instant readability.
- Camera Or Perspective: Front-facing studio cutout view with shallow product overlap and minimal perspective distortion.
- Typography Style: Heavy condensed sans-serif headline, stacked or arched placement, white and yellow type on red fields, small microcopy bands.
- Color Palette Behavior: Red dominates the field, yellow creates structural emphasis, white adds typographic punch, and green or black accents add rhythm.
- Graphic Elements: Rounded panels, arch-like curves, sticker bursts, small tabs, star separators, arrow-like labels, and menu-panel rectangles.
- Texture And Finish: Clean glossy product highlights, print-ready sharpness, mild poster grain, and controlled shadow chips.
- Lighting: Even commercial studio light with bright edge highlights and consistent soft shadows under cutout objects.
- Mood And Cultural Reference: Playful quick-service celebration poster, product-first, direct, loud, and immediately readable.
- Reusable Design Rules: Always build a central trophy silhouette from products, keep the background graphic and flat, and make the headline feel like a retail launch poster.
- Things To Avoid: No official logos, no exact campaign copy, no official trophy replica, no realistic stadium scene, no cluttered lifestyle photography.

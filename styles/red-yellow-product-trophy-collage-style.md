Red Yellow Product Trophy Collage - Design Language

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Palette: Red dominates the field, yellow creates structural emphasis, white adds typographic punch, and green or black accents add rhythm.
- Graphic elements: Rounded panels, arch-like curves, sticker bursts, small tabs, star separators, arrow-like labels, and menu-panel rectangles.
- Texture and finish: Clean glossy product highlights, print-ready sharpness, mild poster grain, and controlled shadow chips.
- Lighting: Even commercial studio light with bright edge highlights and consistent soft shadows under cutout objects.

Jade Glyph Grocer Collage Poster - Design Language

A sparse East Asian grocer-poster system built from warm cream paper, oversized jade-green hand-cut glyphs, pale vegetable silhouette clouds, tiny editorial headers, and one glossy produce-photo centerpiece layered over the typography.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Overall Style Category: Contemporary East Asian fresh-grocer poster collage combining rough green hand-lettering, soft botanical silhouettes, and one photoreal product cutout.
- Composition And Layout: A narrow cream poster field carries a top micro-navigation row, a large irregular glyph field, pale mint silhouette blobs, a large photo object in the lower or side field, and compact bottom information blocks.
- Subject Placement: The product photo should overlap or interrupt the large glyph field, sitting forward like a cutout pasted over flat print graphics.
- Camera Or Perspective: Close product still-life perspective, front-facing or three-quarter, with natural highlights and minimal shadow.
- Typography Style: Huge rough jade-green glyphs with naive brush/cut-paper edges; tiny black editorial labels at top and bottom for contrast.
- Color Palette: Warm cream paper, saturated jade green, pale mint backing shapes, black micro type, and small natural color shifts from the product.
- Graphic Elements: Soft leafy silhouettes, tiny square bullets, short top labels, compact bottom stamps, pinyin-like micro marks, and occasional dot or bracket accents.
- Texture And Finish: Uncoated paper grain, imperfect ink edges, light print softness, and a clean pasted-photo product cutout.
- Lighting: High-key product lighting with glossy highlights; no dramatic cinematic shadows.
- Mood And Reference: Fresh neighborhood produce culture, playful handmade signage, modern editorial poster design, light and friendly.

Market Brush Produce Poster - Design Language

A sparse fresh-market poster system with a warm ivory paper field, one oversized glossy produce hero, a smaller echo product, violent black hand-cut brush typography, thin editorial header text, and tiny bottom seal-like captions.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Overall Style Category: Contemporary East Asian market poster combining photoreal produce collage, editorial product-ad layout, and rough handmade typographic abstraction.
- Composition And Layout: A cream poster field carries one huge low-anchored produce subject, an upper/right block of aggressive black hand lettering, a fine header strip, and a bottom micro-caption strip.
- Subject Placement: The hero produce should be large enough to leave the frame or nearly touch an edge; a second smaller produce echo creates scale play without adding clutter.
- Camera Or Perspective: Close still-life product perspective with shallow depth, soft highlights, and minimal shadow; the page itself remains flat and graphic.
- Typography Style: Two tiers: oversized torn black brush-glyph headline with dry ink edges, and tiny refined editorial captions in sans/serif combinations.
- Color Palette: Ivory paper, black ink, a single vivid fruit or vegetable color, small green botanical accents, and almost no extra hues.
- Graphic Elements: Small stars, parenthesis stamps, thin rules, numbered micro labels, registration marks, and seal-like bottom badges.
- Texture And Finish: Soft print grain, faint paper warmth, slightly diffused product highlights, and rough ink textures in the black type.
- Lighting: High-key studio lighting with white reflective highlights on the produce and no heavy cast shadows.
- Mood And Reference: Fresh market culture, handmade grocery poster, urban editorial food campaign, clean but imperfect.

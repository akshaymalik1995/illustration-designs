Gradient & Glassmorphism Style

Interpret the user's idea and communicate it in the luminous gradient-and-glass language of modern digital product design.

Creative process: express the idea as an elegant arrangement of glowing forms and frosted glass panels — abstract enough to feel premium, concrete enough to communicate.

Every image must feature:

- Smooth multi-stop gradients: vivid but tasteful (violet–blue, coral–peach, teal–mint families)
- Frosted-glass panels: translucent surfaces with background blur and thin light borders
- Soft floating 3D or flat shapes layered at varying depths
- Gentle glow and diffused colored lighting
- Generous dark or softly-lit background that makes gradients luminous
- Subtle grain overlay to prevent banding and add texture
- Floating UI-like cards, orbs, or abstract metaphor objects as fits the concept
- Clean spacious composition with a clear focal cluster

Mood: futuristic, premium, optimistic. The hero-image language of top-tier SaaS and fintech brands.

Avoid: flat matte color only, hard outlines, cluttered layouts, dated skeuomorphism, harsh neon overload.

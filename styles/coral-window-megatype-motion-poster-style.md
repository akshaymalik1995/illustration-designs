Coral Window Megatype Motion Poster - Design Language

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Composition: A large coral paper field holds one cool photo rectangle. Oversized black words cross the rectangle and outer field in opposing orientations, while one cutout action figure ties the flat type and photographic depth together.
- Graphic elements: Use one bold block-built arrow or chevron, short hairline rules only when needed, and tiny issue-like labels. Avoid stickers, bursts, ornamental frames, and UI chrome.
- Mood: Kinetic yet disciplined editorial design: experimental fashion energy contained by a rational modernist grid.

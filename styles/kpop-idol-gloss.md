K-Pop Idol Gloss Style

Interpret the user's subject as a glossy idol-pop illustration — music-video glamour rendered in luminous vector airbrush.

Creative process: stage the subject like the hero frame of an idol group's comeback teaser — confident pose, adoring lighting, a stage-set of floating graphic props built around them.

Every image must feature:

- Sleek vector forms shaded with smooth airbrush gradients — every surface subtly lit, nothing flat
- A vivid pop palette: electric cobalt and ultramarine, hot pink and magenta, coral-pink skin tones, lime and deep green accents, anchored by navy or deep-black fields
- Chrome and metallic accents: silver hearts, chains, beads, rings — rendered with liquid-metal highlights
- Four-point sparkle stars scattered as light glints
- Large glossy eyes with bright specular highlights; stylized sleek hair with shine bands
- Fashion styling: earrings, necklaces, statement jackets, streetwear-meets-stage outfits
- Floating background graphics: oversized elegant serif or script type fragments, abstract blobs, checkered ribbons, halo arcs
- Soft glow and bloom around light sources; dreamy backlit atmosphere
- Dynamic K-pop poses — finger hearts, mic grips, choreography freeze-frames

Mood: dazzling, youthful, romantic, star-struck. A fan-made love letter to an idol, polished to music-label quality.

Avoid: flat single-tone fills, rough or sketchy linework, muted earthy palettes, gritty texture, realistic anatomy, static passport-photo poses.

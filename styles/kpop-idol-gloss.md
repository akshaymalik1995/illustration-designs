K-Pop Idol Gloss Style

This is a STYLIZED VECTOR ILLUSTRATION style — a graphic poster, never a realistic or anime-photoreal render. Think Adobe Illustrator gradient-mesh art for a K-pop fan poster.

Construction rules (these define the style — violate none):

- Everything is built from clean vector shapes with crisp Bézier edges; gradients exist only INSIDE shapes as smooth airbrush fills
- Anatomy is simplified and stylized: elongated smooth limbs, small pointed nose or none, tiny mouth, oversized glossy cartoon eyes with big white specular shapes and colored irises
- Skin is an unnatural candy gradient — coral-pink to peach to magenta — never realistic skin tone or texture
- Hair is a sleek graphic mass in 2–4 sculpted chunks with a bright shine band, colored navy-black, blue, or dyed pop colors
- The scene floats in a GRAPHIC SPACE, not a real place: background built from flat or gradient color fields, oversized abstract blobs, checkerboard ribbons, halo arcs, and fragments of elegant oversized serif type
- Chrome props rendered as cartoon liquid metal: hearts, chains, beads, rings, watering cans — simple shapes with white streak highlights
- Four-point sparkle stars scattered as graphic glints
- Palette: electric cobalt and ultramarine, hot pink and magenta, candy coral, lime or deep green accents, chrome silver, on deep navy or vivid gradient fields
- Fashion styling on figures: earrings, chains, statement jackets, stage-wear
- Poses are confident idol freeze-frames with expressive hands

Mood: dazzling, youthful, star-struck — a music-label comeback teaser as a vector poster.

STRICTLY FORBIDDEN: photorealism, realistic anime rendering, real skies or clouds, photographic depth of field, lens flares, realistic skin/fabric texture, painterly brushwork, soft-focus backgrounds, real-world environments. If the result could be mistaken for a photo or an anime film still, it is wrong.

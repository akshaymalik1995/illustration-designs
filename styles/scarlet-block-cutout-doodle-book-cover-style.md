Scarlet Block Cutout Doodle Book Cover - Design Language

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Composition: The design starts as a spare white book-cover field. Huge scarlet red serif or slab typeforms act as architecture, filling the center and lower frame while being cropped at the edges. A realistic cutout subject sits on top of those letters, mostly vertical, interrupting the red shapes. A rough black hand-drawn contour line is added as a disruptive second skin around the subject. Small serif text is used as furniture, not as a dense text block.
- Texture and finish: White paper grain, print-like red ink, clean photographic object detail, and an uneven black marker line. The line should feel physically drawn over a printed cover.
- Mood: Dry wit, literary absurdity, museum-bookshop design, tactile print, spare but confrontational.

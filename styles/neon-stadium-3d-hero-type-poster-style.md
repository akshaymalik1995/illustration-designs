Neon Stadium 3D Hero Type Poster - Design Language

A hyper-saturated 3D event-poster style with toy-like heroic figures, extreme low-angle action, enormous cropped condensed typography, neon lime and purple color fields, orange-red graphic slashes, stadium light bloom, foreground motion blur, debris, and clean high-resolution tactile surfaces.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Overall Style Category: Glossy 3D animated sports key art mixed with bold commercial event-poster typography.
- Composition Logic: Panoramic action scene with one giant central figure, foreground impact blur, smaller competitors in depth, and headline typography used as structural architecture.
- Subject Placement: One oversized hero dominates nearly half the frame and is cropped by at least one edge; secondary subjects are smaller and staggered along diagonal movement paths.
- Camera Perspective: Low, wide-angle, near-ground camera with exaggerated scale, lens proximity, and strong foreground-to-background depth.
- Typography Style: Ultra-heavy condensed uppercase sans letters, extremely large, tightly cropped, vertically stretched, and layered behind figures.
- Color Palette Behavior: Electric complementary clashes rather than natural sports colors; purple and acid lime dominate with orange-red cuts, turquoise flashes, white glare, and cool sky blue.
- Graphic Elements: Geometric wedges, diagonal slices, cropped color blocks, flare glints, and bottom-corner shards.
- Texture Finish: Clean 3D render with fabric weave, sweat beads, turf or ground particles, dirt, controlled motion blur, and glossy highlights.
- Lighting: Bright outdoor stadium daylight or arena spectacle with floodlight glare, sun bloom, rim highlights, and atmospheric sky depth.
- Mood: Fast, heroic, playful, toyetic, competitive, and high-budget.

Neon Type Photo Scribble Poster - Design Language

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Composition: A flat poster base carries huge cropped black words; a central photo rectangle creates the only depth; white handwriting acts as the top layer.
- Texture and finish: Clean digital poster field with compressed social-media photo grain and soft spray-paint edges in the handwritten mark.
- Mood: Competitive, loud, urban, time-sensitive, and announcement-driven.

Neon Type Photo Scribble Poster - Design Language

A high-impact event poster system built from a toxic neon color field, huge black condensed typography, a centered documentary photo crop, and a raw white spray-marker gesture that cuts across the middle of the composition.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Overall Style Category: Street event flyer collage with documentary photo insertion, oversized typographic architecture, and aerosol handwriting.
- Composition Logic: A flat poster base carries huge cropped black words; a central photo rectangle creates the only depth; white handwriting acts as the top layer.
- Subject Placement: One dominant subject sits in the central photo block, cropped close enough to feel immediate but still surrounded by environmental witnesses.
- Camera Perspective: Eye-level or slightly low documentary photo, realistic lens compression, imperfect live-event timing.
- Texture And Finish: Clean digital poster field with compressed social-media photo grain and soft spray-paint edges in the handwritten mark.
- Mood: Competitive, loud, urban, time-sensitive, and announcement-driven.

Sky Blue Lucky Tag Doodle Poster - Design Language

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Perspective: front-facing flattened poster view with sideways mascot silhouettes, no vanishing point, and playful scale distortion
- Palette: dominant sky blue and off-white structure with black outlines; bright accent fills appear as large clean blocks of orange-red, green, yellow, pink, blue, and brown
- Graphic elements: hanging cords, loop knot, rounded tag, simple stripes, dots, tiny marks, flat mascot body segments, small face details, and short hand-lettered labels
- Texture and finish: clean flat fills, slight poster softness, hand-drawn edge wobble, no visible paper grain as a primary effect, no shading except rare tiny color separations
- Lighting: pure flat graphic lighting; there are no shadows, highlights, or material reflections
- Mood: bright, lucky, playful, handmade, souvenir-like, childlike, friendly, and slightly absurd

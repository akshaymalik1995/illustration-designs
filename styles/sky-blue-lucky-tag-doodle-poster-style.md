Sky Blue Lucky Tag Doodle Poster - Design Language

A sparse flat doodle-poster system built around a saturated sky-blue field, chunky white headline type, an off-white hanging lucky-tag plaque, thick uneven black outlines, bright toy colors, and one large simplified mascot or prop crossing the tag like a playful charm illustration.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Overall Style Category: flat lucky-tag doodle poster, naive mascot charm illustration, sparse souvenir-card graphic system
- Composition And Layout: a full sky-blue field carries a large white headline at the top, a central off-white hanging tag below, a cord-and-knot accent near the tag top, and one large flat mascot or prop angled across the lower tag
- Subject Placement: the subject is oversized and centered low, often crossing the tag boundary or cropping at the edge; supporting marks stay minimal and secondary
- Camera Angle Or Perspective: front-facing flattened poster view with sideways mascot silhouettes, no vanishing point, and playful scale distortion
- Typography Style: top text uses heavy rounded white block letters with hand-cut irregularity; secondary text uses loose black marker handwriting on the tag face
- Color Palette Behavior: dominant sky blue and off-white structure with black outlines; bright accent fills appear as large clean blocks of orange-red, green, yellow, pink, blue, and brown
- Graphic Elements: hanging cords, loop knot, rounded tag, simple stripes, dots, tiny marks, flat mascot body segments, small face details, and short hand-lettered labels
- Texture And Finish: clean flat fills, slight poster softness, hand-drawn edge wobble, no visible paper grain as a primary effect, no shading except rare tiny color separations
- Lighting: pure flat graphic lighting; there are no shadows, highlights, or material reflections
- Mood: bright, lucky, playful, handmade, souvenir-like, childlike, friendly, and slightly absurd

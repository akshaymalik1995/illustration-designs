Cream Smoke City Manga Poster - Design Language

A vertical and horizontal illustration system built from fine manga ink contour lines, huge cream-toned organic cloud masses, sparse teal framing shapes, peach skin or object accents, and precise miniature urban architecture rendered with clean paper space.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Overall Style Category: Surreal editorial manga poster illustration with flat poster space, precise ink drawing, and dense organic linework.
- Composition And Layout: One oversized central figure or object anchors the middle, an enormous cream organic mass rises or sweeps above it, and small architectural blocks form a compressed city-like base.
- Subject Placement: The subject is close-cropped and centered or slightly low in frame, with the largest visual mass hovering above or around the upper half.
- Camera Or Perspective: Low-angle portrait framing for people and shallow isometric or front-on geometry for buildings and props.
- Typography Style: No strong headline typography in the reference; any text should appear only as tiny hand-drawn signage or restrained microcopy.
- Color Palette Behavior: The image depends on warm neutral paper and cream forms, with muted teal as the dominant contrast and coral or peach as a small accent.
- Graphic Elements: Curled puffs, droplets, small chips, stipple patches, window grids, hatch fills, and a sweeping flat teal boundary.
- Texture And Finish: Clean digital ink with subtle paper warmth, light gray shadows, selective stipple, and no dirty digital noise.
- Lighting: Soft ambient poster light with minimal cast shadow and gentle tonal patches.
- Mood And Reference: Quiet, wonder-struck, slightly anxious urban surrealism with Japanese manga poster sensitivity.

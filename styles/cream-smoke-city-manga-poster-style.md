Cream Smoke City Manga Poster - Design Language

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Palette: The image depends on warm neutral paper and cream forms, with muted teal as the dominant contrast and coral or peach as a small accent.
- Graphic elements: Curled puffs, droplets, small chips, stipple patches, window grids, hatch fills, and a sweeping flat teal boundary.
- Texture and finish: Clean digital ink with subtle paper warmth, light gray shadows, selective stipple, and no dirty digital noise.
- Lighting: Soft ambient poster light with minimal cast shadow and gentle tonal patches.

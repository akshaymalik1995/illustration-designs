Ice Cyan Megatype Action Poster - Design Language

A kinetic editorial action poster style built from an ice-white canvas, oversized deep-cyan condensed typography, pale gray ghost text layers, a central cutout action photograph, neon chartreuse motion blurs, small barcode and registration marks, and sparse magazine-style microcopy.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Style Category: High-energy sports and culture editorial poster with photographic action cutout and oversized typographic collage.
- Layout: Mostly white vertical poster field with stacked oversize cyan type through the center, pale gray repeated ghost type behind it, and one action subject breaking forward over both layers.
- Subject Placement: The subject is centered slightly off-axis, enlarged, sharply cut out, and allowed to cover headline letters while one body part or prop projects toward the viewer.
- Camera Perspective: Low or front-close editorial action perspective with strong foreshortening, dynamic diagonal body lines, and clean commercial lighting.
- Texture Finish: Clean digital editorial finish with crisp type, subtle print softness, controlled drop shadow, and soft neon blur accents.
- Mood: Competitive, energetic, aspirational, magazine-like, technical, and polished.

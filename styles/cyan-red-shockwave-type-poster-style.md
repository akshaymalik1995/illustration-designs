Cyan Red Shockwave Type Poster - Design Language

A flat illustrated poster system built from a saturated cyan field, oversized red block typography, lemon-yellow human accents, white jagged shockwaves, thick red keylines, and compact rotated microcopy. It feels like a loud regional travel poster crossed with manga impact graphics and screenprinted street signage.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Style Category: Flat pop poster illustration with techno-manga impact graphics and regional sign-paint typography.
- Layout: A full-bleed cyan field is packed with cropped red display type, a diagonal white shockwave, a large central prop, and small rotated caption islands.
- Subject System: One central object or action is enlarged to poster scale, held or pushed by simplified yellow limbs or surrounded by graphic punctuation.
- Perspective: Mostly orthographic flatness with diagonal slashes and forced motion replacing natural camera perspective.
- Texture: Clean screenprint-like color fields, hard vector edges, minimal print texture, and only tiny controlled gradients.
- Mood: Noisy, celebratory, fast, urban, humorous, and commercial without becoming photoreal or cluttered.

Sun-Faded Scenic Editorial Poster - Design Language

A nostalgic scenic editorial poster system that combines enormous warm-ivory condensed headlines, one flowing tangerine script accent, tiny travel-magazine microcopy, and a low-angle analog photograph beneath a broad cyan sky with sun-faded film grain.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Overall Style Category: Sun-faded mid-century travel-editorial poster combining display typography with an analog scenic photograph.
- Composition Logic: A narrow top-caption strip leads into two huge stacked headline rows; a script accent bridges them; a small centered subtitle marks the transition to a full-width photograph in the lower half.
- Subject Placement: One recognizable subject sits in the lower-middle, large enough to read instantly but surrounded by ground and a thin landscape horizon.
- Camera Perspective: Low or near-ground wide-angle view, eye line close to the subject's lower half, with a level horizon and abundant open sky.
- Texture Finish: Printed analog-film finish with fine grain, mild color fade, slightly chalky type, soft bloom, tiny dust, and restrained paper speckle.

Rough Animation Pet Sketch Storyboard - Design Language

A loose animation development sketch style for comic pet scenes, built from warm beige paper, red-brown construction lines, scratchy dark burgundy contours, semi-transparent color wash, simple room props, and exaggerated animal expressions.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Style Category: A loose animation development sketch style for comic pet scenes, built from warm beige paper, red-brown construction lines, scratchy dark burgundy contours, semi-transparent color wash, simple room props, and exaggerated animal expressions.
- Composition Logic: A reusable poster/image system where the subject, prop, location, text, and accent variables change while the layout grammar remains fixed.
- Subject Treatment: The subject is driven by SUBJECT and SUBJECT_ACTION, with wardrobe or visual treatment supplied by WARDROBE_STYLE.
- Background System: LOCATION and BACKGROUND_ELEMENTS provide the scene, texture, spatial cues, and secondary details without changing the style family.
- Type And Graphics: MAIN_TEXT, SECONDARY_TEXT, and ACCENT_SYMBOL control readable text, microcopy, stickers, marks, arrows, bursts, or other graphic accents.
- Content Boundary: The negative prompt and source_content_to_avoid list prevent direct recreation of source subjects, marks, brands, watermarks, and story premises.

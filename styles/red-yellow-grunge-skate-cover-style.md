Red Yellow Grunge Skate Cover - Design Language

A raw underground action-culture magazine cover style with oversized warped red-and-yellow headline type, flash-lit photographic cutouts, dark tilted street or venue backgrounds, dense boxed callouts, and coarse analog print degradation.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Overall Style Category: Punk action-culture photo collage cover, vintage zine scan, and underground event flyer.
- Composition Logic: Giant top masthead, one cutout action subject crossing the masthead, tilted dark photographic background, and dense lower callout blocks.
- Subject Placement: A single flash-lit subject or hero object is scaled large enough to overlap the title and scene, usually upper center or upper right.
- Camera Perspective: Low-angle or tilted wide-lens background with direct-flash snapshot subject and mild fisheye distortion.
- Typography Style: All-caps warped display lettering with chunky slab or bubble contours, red fill, yellow stroke, rough edges, tight spacing, and stacked supporting copy.
- Color Behavior: Hot red and yellow carry the graphic system while black and cool blue-gray hold the photographic scene.
- Texture Finish: Scanned paper grain, photocopy noise, halftone dirt, soft ink spread, registration drift, and worn edges.
- Mood: Rowdy, nostalgic, reckless, noisy, handmade, and collectible.

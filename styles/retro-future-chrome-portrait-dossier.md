Retro Future Chrome Portrait Dossier - Design Language

A retro-futurist editorial portrait system built from an asymmetric technical dossier sidebar, an edge-cropped posterized face, liquid-chrome interruptions, modular grid lines, optical diagrams, and coarse halftone print grain in midnight indigo, warm cream, hot magenta, cobalt, and coral.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Overall Style Category: Retro-futurist cyber-editorial portrait poster with technical dossier graphics and halftone screen-print texture.
- Composition And Layout: A dense asymmetric two-column page: a narrow stacked information rail on one side and a dominant close-up portrait on the other, both locked together by thin grid lines and framed modules.
- Subject Placement: The portrait fills nearly the entire hero field and is cut by the top, side, and bottom edges; the eye and mouth create the primary diagonal reading path.
- Camera Or Perspective: Extreme close-up with a slight low or oblique angle, flattened into graphic illustration while chrome pieces retain exaggerated reflective depth.
- Typography Style: One large italic extended techno headline, tiny condensed dossier labels, sparse handwritten annotation, and abstract microcopy used as texture.
- Color Palette Behavior: A dark indigo ground supports warm cream figure planes; magenta and cobalt shadows alternate against coral and pale-yellow accents, with no neutral photographic color.
- Graphic Elements: Hairline grids, registration crosses, wireframe meshes, optical waves, star sparks, small specimen panels, contour terrains, and chrome interruptions.
- Texture And Finish: Coarse printed halftone, paper grain, screen-print speckle, mild ink trapping, and slightly imperfect color registration.
- Lighting: Hard graphic highlight planes and saturated shadow blocks instead of realistic illumination; chrome carries sharp multi-color reflections.
- Mood And Cultural Reference: Futuristic fashion editorial, speculative interface manual, Y2K rave flyer, and anime-influenced beauty poster.
- Reusable Design Rules: Keep the sidebar functional and diagrammatic, make the portrait monumental, route chrome elements through the portrait diagonally, and bind all parts with the same grid and print texture.
- Things To Avoid: No photoreal portrait, clean vector minimalism, soft pastel lifestyle scene, cinematic 3D environment, exact source face, copied text, or literal source diagrams.

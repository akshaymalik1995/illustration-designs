Dusk Cyan Layered Type Poster - Design Language

A full-bleed dusk-photo poster system with dark navy silhouettes, oversized cyan and white typography, a huge cropped bottom word, script-like swashes, tiny editorial microcopy, and clean vector icon overlays.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Style Category: Photographic editorial poster with maximal layered typography and clean vector graphics.
- Composition: Full-bleed background, heavy central title block, cropped oversized bottom word, and small text islands at corners.
- Subject Placement: The scene acts as atmospheric background while typography becomes the dominant foreground subject.
- Perspective: Mostly flat poster composition over a real photo, with shallow scene depth and minimal perspective emphasis.
- Mood: Calm, cinematic, travel-editorial, nostalgic, and typographically assertive.

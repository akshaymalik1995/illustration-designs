Sky Blue Home Life Doodle Poster - Design Language

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Perspective: front-facing flattened poster perspective with childlike scale distortion and no strict vanishing point
- Palette: dominant sky blue and off-white structure with black outlines; secondary colors appear as flat blocks of coral red, yellow, teal, green, beige, and soft pink
- Graphic elements: house outline, red badge, slanted date-like mark, hash marks, simple shelf, sofa, lamp, rug grid, plant pot, book blocks, small pet silhouettes, and casual marker ticks
- Texture and finish: clean flat fills with mild marker wobble, hand-colored edges, imperfect black strokes, simple poster softness, and no high-detail material rendering
- Lighting: flat graphic lighting; illumination is symbolized by a drawn lamp or sun mark rather than realistic shadows
- Mood: playful, youthful, homey, handmade, lightly absurd, relaxed, and event-like

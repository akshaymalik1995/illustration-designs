Sky Blue Home Life Doodle Poster - Design Language

A naive flat poster system with a full sky-blue field, a rough house-shaped white inset, giant uneven black hand-lettering, small badge labels, and simple thick-outlined cartoon home-life scenes drawn like marker doodles.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Overall Style Category: naive home-life event poster, flat cartoon doodle flyer, hand-lettered domestic illustration
- Composition And Layout: a sky-blue poster field frames a white house-shaped interior vignette; the top third is dominated by large black hand-lettering and small event labels; the center and lower area hold simple home props and chunky cartoon subjects
- Subject Placement: one or two original figures, pets, or large props occupy the lower half, often cropped by the frame or overlapping the house inset for a playful foreground effect
- Camera Angle Or Perspective: front-facing flattened poster perspective with childlike scale distortion and no strict vanishing point
- Typography Style: huge irregular hand-drawn black headline, mixed with tiny slanted support text and a red rounded capsule badge; text looks drawn, not typeset
- Color Palette Behavior: dominant sky blue and off-white structure with black outlines; secondary colors appear as flat blocks of coral red, yellow, teal, green, beige, and soft pink
- Graphic Elements: house outline, red badge, slanted date-like mark, hash marks, simple shelf, sofa, lamp, rug grid, plant pot, book blocks, small pet silhouettes, and casual marker ticks
- Texture And Finish: clean flat fills with mild marker wobble, hand-colored edges, imperfect black strokes, simple poster softness, and no high-detail material rendering
- Lighting: flat graphic lighting; illumination is symbolized by a drawn lamp or sun mark rather than realistic shadows
- Mood: playful, youthful, homey, handmade, lightly absurd, relaxed, and event-like

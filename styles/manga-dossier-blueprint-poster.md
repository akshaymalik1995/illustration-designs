Manga Dossier Blueprint Poster - Design Language

A distressed manga character-dossier poster system with cream paper margins, oversized condensed typography, grayscale ink hero portraits, cobalt-blue technical panels, cyan kinetic effects, small inset frames, catalog labels, and editorial annotation rails.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Overall Style Category: Distressed manga editorial dossier poster with technical blueprint accents and collectible character-card information design.
- Composition And Layout: Tall poster logic with a large portrait occupying the upper center/right, huge stacked typography on the left, small profile-copy blocks and number labels beneath, inset monochrome frames near the lower left, and vertical side annotation along the outer edge. The horizontal variant should preserve the same dossier hierarchy by stretching the title band and placing portrait, inset panels, and action figure across a wider editorial spread.
- Subject Placement: Hero subject is cropped large, usually from chest or shoulders upward, with a secondary smaller action pose crossing the foreground; both figures overlap a strong blue rectangular field and printed metadata.
- Camera Or Perspective: Mostly flat poster perspective with slight low-angle or three-quarter manga portrait view; the smaller figure can use dynamic foreshortening but remains ink-drawn rather than realistic.
- Typography Style: Tall condensed grotesque headline, heavy black and blue blocks, weathered ink, all caps, tight line spacing, tiny uppercase labels, small paragraph text, narrow side rails, and number-box typography.
- Color Palette Behavior: Cream paper and monochrome ink dominate; cobalt blue creates the structural panel and secondary title color; cyan is reserved for kinetic paint-like strokes; accents remain restrained.
- Graphic Elements: Blueprint panel, small emblems, catalog number box, barcode-like stripes, profile text blocks, inset image frames, thin rules, vertical annotations, registration ticks, faded technical marks, and painted energy strokes.
- Texture And Finish: Aged paper, screenprint distress, dust, ink wear, photocopied gray values, faint halftone, rough panel edges, and dry-brush energy marks.
- Lighting: Flat editorial print contrast, cel-shaded grayscale forms, no realistic cast shadows, no rim-lit cinema glow except cyan graphic energy.
- Mood And Cultural Reference: Collectible manga profile poster, tactical dossier, premium fan-zine editorial, and worn screenprinted character archive.

Acid Lime 3D Streetwear Type Poster - Design Language

A glossy C4D streetwear campaign poster system built from pale studio space, oversized black block typography, acid-lime marker strokes, sticker badges, fashion-tool props, low wide-angle character staging, and clean synthetic product lighting.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Style Category: Glossy C4D youth-fashion poster, streetwear product campaign, acid-lime type-and-avatar social ad.
- Composition Logic: A large black headline mass occupies the left or upper area while a polished 3D subject cluster occupies the opposite side. Small stickers, tools, and props bridge the type and character zones.
- Subject Placement: The subject sits, crouches, leans, kneels, or poses close to camera, with one oversized foreground object creating depth and scale.
- Camera Angle Or Perspective: Low front three-quarter wide-angle perspective with mild lens exaggeration and cropped foreground props.
- Typography Style: Huge black geometric block letters, condensed and tightly stacked, with short secondary type in black or acid-lime capsules; type behaves like a physical campaign wall.
- Color Palette Behavior: A pale neutral base carries black type and acid-lime accents; small hot pink, yellow, orange, or cyan stickers add pop without overtaking the lime.
- Graphic Elements: Marker strokes, signature-like scribbles, barcode strips, warning labels, tiny capsules, scalloped sticker seals, round smiley badges, hang tags, and thin black connector lines.
- Texture And Finish: Smooth plastic skin, matte-black rubber, glossy chrome, soft textile weave, semi-translucent acrylic, sticker paper, and crisp printed type.
- Lighting: High-key studio lighting, soft floor shadows, gentle specular highlights, and clean advertising contrast.
- Mood: Fashion-forward, playful, slightly cyber, youth pop-up, collectible, polished, and energetic.

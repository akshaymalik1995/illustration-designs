Y2K Streetwear Sticker Collage - Design Language

A dense Y2K street-photo collage style with oversized cutout subjects, sticker props, loud comic typography, speech bubbles, saturated yellow-blue-green accents, and controlled high-contrast social-poster texture.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Style Category: Photographic Y2K streetwear collage poster with comic title treatment and sticker-prop overlays.
- Layout: A large cutout subject dominates the frame while inset panels, foreground props, speech bubbles, and giant type crowd the remaining space.
- Perspective: Casual wide-angle street-camera perspective, slightly tilted, with shallow urban depth and close foreground objects.
- Texture: High-contrast compressed social-image sharpness, glossy photo surfaces, controlled print grain, and clean sticker edges.
- Mood: Loud, playful, prankish, street-culture coded, and game-like.

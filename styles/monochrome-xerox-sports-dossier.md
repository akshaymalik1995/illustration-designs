Monochrome Xerox Sports Dossier - Design Language

A black-and-white photocopied editorial collage system with oversized cropped subjects, overlapping inset photo panels, huge distressed condensed type, micro dossier labels, paper grain, halftone, scratches, and a sparse sports-press-kit mood.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Style Category: monochrome editorial photo-collage sports poster with xerox print degradation
- Layout: dominant cropped photo panel, smaller overlapping inset panels, large lower headline, micro labels, wide paper margin
- Subject Placement: main figure is oversized, close-cropped, and slightly left or center weighted; secondary panels stack on one side
- Camera Perspective: editorial portrait or action photography flattened into scanned print panels, with no deep perspective illusion
- Finish: photocopy grain, halftone, dust, scratches, ink drag, rough paper texture, and distressed type edges

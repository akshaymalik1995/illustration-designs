Sunny 3D Avatar Campaign - Design Language

A bright social campaign poster style built from glossy toy-like 3D avatars, saturated blue-sky outdoor lighting, exaggerated wide-angle perspective, oversized slanted headline typography, and neon hand-drawn motion marks.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Composition Logic: A mobile-first poster with a large sky field for type, a tilted wide-angle camera, a dominant foreground avatar or prop, secondary avatar or product detail in the midground, and a dense but readable commercial footer.
- Subject Placement: The main figure is oversized and close to camera, often cropped at the edges. Supporting characters, products, and props sit behind the lead subject to create scale and depth.
- Camera Perspective: Low, close, wide-angle perspective with mild fisheye distortion; foreground objects feel touchable and larger than life.
- Typography Behavior: Huge white block letters occupy the upper third or half, slanted with compressed spacing and accompanied by smaller secondary type. The text behaves like a bold campaign title, not a quiet caption.
- Graphic Elements: Neon marker loops, search-like badges, rounded labels, tiny stickers, and motion strokes create a social-platform campaign grammar without using actual platform marks.
- Texture And Surface: Smooth 3D surfaces, soft fabric weave on clothing, shiny plastic or metal props, dewy grass or clean outdoor surfaces, and polished advertising contrast.
- Lighting Mood: Optimistic midday sunlight with saturated color, crisp shadows, and a fresh summer-release mood.

Sunny 3D Avatar Campaign - Design Language

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Composition: A mobile-first poster with a large sky field for type, a tilted wide-angle camera, a dominant foreground avatar or prop, secondary avatar or product detail in the midground, and a dense but readable commercial footer.
- Graphic elements: Neon marker loops, search-like badges, rounded labels, tiny stickers, and motion strokes create a social-platform campaign grammar without using actual platform marks.

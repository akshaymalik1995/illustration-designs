Blue Chinese Perspective Type Canyon - Design Language

A Chinese typographic poster system built from an extreme one-point perspective corridor: a saturated blue central trapezoid plane carries stacked oversized white Chinese display type, while black side walls are packed with warped white and pale gray Chinese support copy.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Composition Logic: The image is built as a Chinese typographic tunnel. The central plane is a saturated blue tapered rectangle in one-point perspective; the side planes are black walls angled toward a narrow vanishing point. Cropped Chinese letterforms at the edges increase the sense of scale.
- Subject Placement: The subject is mostly typographic. The biggest Chinese glyph or numeric element sits near the lower foreground, while the headline and smaller supporting blocks recede upward along the blue center plane.
- Camera Perspective: Low, close, wide-angle one-point perspective with converging vertical side walls and a compressed vanishing area near the upper center.
- Typographic Behavior: Bold Chinese display glyphs, heavy geometric numerals, and compact Chinese support copy are treated as architecture. Type is skewed, stacked, cropped, and repeated.
- Surface Behavior: Planes are clean and flat, but white letters carry light gray shading or beveled tonal variation that makes them read as flat 3D signage.
- Mood: Urgent, loud, compressed, retail-advertising energy with a blue central sign corridor instead of the source red corridor.

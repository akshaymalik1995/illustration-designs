Hot Ink Comic Poster - Design Language

A loud flat comic flyer style built from a mustard-yellow field, coral-red cutout figures, heavy irregular black marker outlines, hand-lettered bubble type, scribbled microcopy, and dense comic symbols. It feels like an underground club poster or xeroxed street handbill, but the reusable system changes the subject, text, props, and story completely.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Overall Style Category: Flat hand-drawn comic poster, street flyer, low-tech screenprint, and doodle typography hybrid.
- Composition Logic: A large red simplified subject anchors the page while black lettering, speech shapes, bursts, arrows, and microcopy orbit the edges.
- Subject Placement: The subject is oversized, front-facing or simple profile, placed near center with limbs or props breaking into surrounding text zones.
- Perspective: No camera realism. Forms are orthographic, emblem-like, and cut from flat color fields.
- Texture: The finish should feel marker-drawn and printed, with uneven strokes, rough text edges, and occasional registration wobble.
- Mood: Playful, noisy, street-level, handmade, and slightly absurd.

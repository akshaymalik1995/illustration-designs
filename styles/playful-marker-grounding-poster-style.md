Playful Marker Grounding Poster - Design Language

A naive hand-drawn poster style built from cream paper margins, loose marker-scribble color blocks, thick uneven keylines, oversized casual lettering, and simple mascot-like figures arranged with clear public-service poster hierarchy.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Style Category: naive marker poster illustration with public-service announcement hierarchy
- Composition Logic: inset poster field with headline-heavy top, huge simplified central subject, sparse supporting doodles, and a small secondary text block near one lower corner
- Subject Placement: main subject is very large and simple, cropped or stretched across the center-lower area, while small objects float nearby for humor and scale
- Perspective: front-facing flat drawing with almost no perspective; scale is emotional and graphic rather than realistic
- Typography: hand-drawn brush-marker lettering, irregular stroke thickness, uneven baseline, dark-brown headline mass, and compact corner microcopy
- Palette Behavior: cream paper plus high-saturation cyan and lime scribbles, dark brown headline, black keylines, white fills, and small pastel accent marks
- Texture: visible marker strokes, unfinished scribble edges, accidental gaps, simple rough paper feel, and no digital polish
- Lighting: flat even color without shadows or dimensional highlights
- Mood: playful, handmade, childlike, friendly, and slightly silly

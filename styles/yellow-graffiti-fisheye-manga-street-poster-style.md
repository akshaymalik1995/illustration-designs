Yellow Graffiti Fisheye Manga Street Poster - Design Language

A hybrid street-poster system combining a cool fisheye urban photo base, a large manga ink cutout subject, oversized sprayed yellow graffiti type, compact white caption hits, pavement tag texture, and soft anxious character emotion.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Overall Style Category: Hybrid fisheye street-photo manga poster, urban sticker-zine cover, and graffiti-tag social graphic.
- Composition And Layout: A wide-angle street photograph fills the frame. A single oversized illustrated manga subject is centered in front, the top is dominated by loose yellow graffiti lettering, small white captions cross the middle, and a large pavement tag anchors the bottom.
- Subject Placement: The foreground subject is close and top-down, with a large head and hands near the face or chest, body tapering toward small feet on the pavement. The subject overlaps the background machines and partially interrupts the graffiti headline.
- Camera Or Perspective: Extreme wide-angle or fisheye street-camera perspective, looking downward from slightly above the subject, with road stripes, curb edges, and ground lettering curving into depth.
- Typography Style: Thick hand-sprayed yellow headline tags with dot overspray, compact white block captions, and chalky pavement graffiti. Text feels painted into the scene or printed as zine overlay rather than typeset cleanly.
- Color Palette Behavior: Desaturated blue-gray city photo tones carry the background. The subject adds warm cream and peach. Electric yellow and white are the only loud graphic accents.
- Graphic Elements: Spray-paint headline, white caption hits, chalky floor tag, rough overspray dots, casual sticker-like accent marks, and hard collage separation between photo and inked character.
- Texture And Finish: Real asphalt grain, vending-machine reflections, compressed street-photo detail, smooth anime ink lines, blush hatch marks, spray-paint speckle, and rough pavement paint.
- Lighting: Overcast outdoor light with cool machine glow; flat enough to keep the manga subject legible, with no dramatic rim light or cinematic depth of field.
- Mood And Cultural Reference: Contemporary Japanese street-snapshot energy, informal graffiti culture, fan-zine cover composition, and manga emotional closeup.

Yellow Graffiti Fisheye Manga Street Poster - Design Language

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Palette: Desaturated blue-gray city photo tones carry the background. The subject adds warm cream and peach. Electric yellow and white are the only loud graphic accents.
- Graphic elements: Spray-paint headline, white caption hits, chalky floor tag, rough overspray dots, casual sticker-like accent marks, and hard collage separation between photo and inked character.
- Texture and finish: Real asphalt grain, vending-machine reflections, compressed street-photo detail, smooth anime ink lines, blush hatch marks, spray-paint speckle, and rough pavement paint.
- Lighting: Overcast outdoor light with cool machine glow; flat enough to keep the manga subject legible, with no dramatic rim light or cinematic depth of field.

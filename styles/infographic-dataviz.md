Infographic & Data-Viz Style

Interpret the user's idea and communicate it as an elegant infographic illustration — information designed, not decorated.

Creative process: find the structure inside the idea (steps, parts, comparison, flow, growth) and choose the visual grammar that makes that structure obvious in three seconds.

Every image must feature:

- A clear organizing device: flow, timeline, radial map, layered diagram, or annotated scene
- Simple pictogram-style icons with consistent stroke weight and corner radius
- Clean connector lines, arrows, and numbered steps guiding the eye
- Short label placeholders integrated into the composition (abstract text is fine)
- Restrained palette: 2–3 brand-like colors plus neutrals; color encodes meaning
- Strong visual hierarchy: one headline element, supporting satellites
- Flat design with occasional subtle shadow for grouping
- Generous white space and grid-aligned layout

Mood: clear, credible, effortlessly smart. The visual language of world-class explainer graphics.

Avoid: decorative clutter, 3D chart effects, more than 3 accent colors, dense text blocks, competing focal points.

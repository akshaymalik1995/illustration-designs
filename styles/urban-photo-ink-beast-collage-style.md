Urban Photo Ink Beast Collage - Design Language

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Palette: Muted blue-gray, tan, and brick photographic color sits beneath dominant black-and-white drawings, with small cobalt, vermilion, lemon, and blush interventions.
- Graphic elements: Airborne rod-and-loop glyphs, motion curls, wiry internal scribbles, repeated dash textures, blunt paper-white highlights, colored eye and mouth masks, and one long ribbon-like yellow accent.
- Texture and finish: The photo is lightly faded and softly grainy; drawn layers stay opaque and sharp but retain uneven ink edges, hand pressure, and small registration-like irregularities.
- Lighting: Natural ambient daylight belongs only to the photo; illustrated figures use flat local color with almost no cast shadows or volumetric shading.

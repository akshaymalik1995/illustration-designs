Red Black Manga Tabloid Poster - Design Language

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Palette: Monochrome dominates, scarlet appears as aggressive spot color for attention, danger, motion, panels, and small marks.
- Graphic elements: Panel frames, tiny icons, splatters, vertical stripes, spiral fields, rating boxes, label bars, stars, crosses, and micro-metadata.
- Texture and finish: Wrinkled paper, photocopy grain, halftone screens, uneven ink, slightly distressed edges, and printed-poster roughness.
- Lighting: Flat editorial lighting with no cinematic gradients or physical cast shadows.

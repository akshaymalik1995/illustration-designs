Red Black Manga Tabloid Poster - Design Language

A dense black, white, and scarlet manga tabloid poster system with oversized condensed type, cropped ink characters, editorial metadata blocks, halftone shading, red spot-color shock graphics, and folded photocopy paper texture.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Overall Style Category: Manga editorial zine poster, photocopied character dossier, punk tabloid spread, and graphic fan-magazine cover.
- Composition And Layout: Asymmetrical but grid-driven collage with a huge headline block, large central illustration, surrounding rectangular manga panels, compact paragraphs, rating labels, icon rows, and a secondary headline zone.
- Subject Placement: The main figure or object should be cropped large and close, overlapping panels and type, with smaller supporting crops placed around it like evidence fragments.
- Camera Or Perspective: Mostly flat frontal or three-quarter manga views with shallow paper depth; detail comes from crops and panels rather than realistic camera depth.
- Typography Style: Black ultra-bold condensed sans display type, compact uppercase label text, tiny paragraph blocks, and spaced title glyphs.
- Color Palette Behavior: Monochrome dominates, scarlet appears as aggressive spot color for attention, danger, motion, panels, and small marks.
- Graphic Elements: Panel frames, tiny icons, splatters, vertical stripes, spiral fields, rating boxes, label bars, stars, crosses, and micro-metadata.
- Texture And Finish: Wrinkled paper, photocopy grain, halftone screens, uneven ink, slightly distressed edges, and printed-poster roughness.
- Lighting: Flat editorial lighting with no cinematic gradients or physical cast shadows.
- Mood And Cultural Reference: Tense, rebellious, hyper-graphic, underground manga magazine energy with restricted dossier attitude.

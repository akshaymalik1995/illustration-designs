Rough Ink Music Doodle Poster - Design Language

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Perspective: mostly flat poster perspective with deliberately naive scale shifts; instruments, bursts, props, and bodies tilt without realistic depth
- Palette: pale blush ground carries the whole poster; deep green-black controls linework and headline; hot pink fills garments, type, and props; teal green acts as a secondary flat fill; acidic yellow appears only as sharp starburst emphasis
- Graphic elements: music notes, starbursts, bracket labels, small capsules, steam lines, motion ticks, scribble curls, floating punctuation, and loose contour marks
- Texture and finish: dry marker edge, scuffed ink, imperfect overprint, speckled fills, rough paper grain, and slightly misregistered color blocks
- Lighting: flat graphic lighting with no realistic shadows; contrast comes from dark ink against pale paper and small saturated color hits
- Mood: playful, handmade, slightly absurd, cozy, music-driven, wellness-themed, zine-like, and informal

Rough Ink Music Doodle Poster - Design Language

A rough hand-inked poster style built from oversized dark green-black brush lettering, pale blush paper, hot pink secondary type, naive mascot drawings, teal and pink flat fills, sharp yellow burst marks, scattered music-note doodles, and scanned risograph-like print texture.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Overall Style Category: rough hand-inked risograph music poster, naive wellness flyer, underground doodle event graphic
- Composition And Layout: large brush-lettered headline dominates the upper area, while independent doodle vignettes float through the middle and lower frame; small pink text blocks live at edges and near props; negative space remains visible between clusters
- Subject Placement: subjects are simplified mascots, performers, or ritual props placed as separate cutout-like drawings; one or two figures can sit or lean low in the frame while smaller objects hover nearby
- Camera Angle Or Perspective: mostly flat poster perspective with deliberately naive scale shifts; instruments, bursts, props, and bodies tilt without realistic depth
- Typography Style: huge rough dark green-black brush characters for the main headline; small hot pink condensed serif-like or hand-rendered labels; occasional bracketed micro labels as graphic marks
- Color Palette Behavior: pale blush ground carries the whole poster; deep green-black controls linework and headline; hot pink fills garments, type, and props; teal green acts as a secondary flat fill; acidic yellow appears only as sharp starburst emphasis
- Graphic Elements: music notes, starbursts, bracket labels, small capsules, steam lines, motion ticks, scribble curls, floating punctuation, and loose contour marks
- Texture And Finish: dry marker edge, scuffed ink, imperfect overprint, speckled fills, rough paper grain, and slightly misregistered color blocks
- Lighting: flat graphic lighting with no realistic shadows; contrast comes from dark ink against pale paper and small saturated color hits
- Mood: playful, handmade, slightly absurd, cozy, music-driven, wellness-themed, zine-like, and informal

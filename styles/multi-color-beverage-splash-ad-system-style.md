Multi-Color Beverage Splash Ad System - Design Language

A reusable beverage launch advertising system with four color-varied templates, built from giant white 3D typography, a diagonal hero drink pack, frozen liquid motion, dense launch-ad copy, and polished commercial product lighting.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Composition Logic: A strong beverage-category color fills the field, giant white extruded letters create dimensional advertising architecture, and one drink pack bursts diagonally forward through frozen liquid motion.
- Subject Placement: The drink pack sits near the center third, cropped large, tilted diagonally, and layered between foreground splash and background type.
- Camera Perspective: Close commercial product-ad perspective with slight wide-angle energy, high depth, and strong retail-impact scale.
- Typography Behavior: Huge white condensed italic letters act as physical stage pieces while headline text stays stacked, short, and channel-readable.
- Color Behavior: The source's monochrome green discipline becomes a controlled per-template palette, with white 3D type as the constant visual anchor.
- Delivery Behavior: 9:16 favors store posters, fridge stickers, and Xiaohongshu covers; 16:9 favors subway lightboxes, brand KV, and retail display backdrops.

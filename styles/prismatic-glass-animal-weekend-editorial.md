Prismatic Glass Animal Weekend Editorial - Design Language

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Palette: Approximately 82 percent black, 11 percent smoky transparent gray and silver, 5 percent spectral cyan-magenta-orange reflections, and 2 percent white typography.
- Graphic elements: No frames or illustrations; the graphic grammar comes from disciplined text clusters, short rules of microcopy, issue numerals, location tags, and small separators.
- Texture and finish: Ultra-clean CGI with polished glass, liquid-metal reflections, sharp caustics, smooth surfaces, and pristine digital typography.
- Lighting: Multiple narrow studio rim lights and colored strip reflections carve the silhouette while deep blacks preserve negative space.

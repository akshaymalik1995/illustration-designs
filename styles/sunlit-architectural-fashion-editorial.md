Sunlit Architectural Fashion Editorial - Design Language

A polished outdoor high-fashion editorial photography style built from low-angle architectural framing, hard Mediterranean daylight, warm stone neutrals, elongated model silhouettes, restrained luxury styling, and fine graphic lines from wires, railings, shadows, or facade geometry. It recreates the premium fashion-campaign feeling without copying the original models, wardrobe, plaza, prop placement, or poses.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Composition Logic: The reference uses a low viewpoint and a close architectural mass on one side to make the models feel tall, expensive, and slightly monumental. The composition is asymmetrical: a large tactile wall or column anchors the foreground, while open sky and distant facades create depth.
- Subject Placement: Subjects sit low and large in the frame, often near the bottom third, with one figure closer and more dominant while the second figure stands farther back and slightly higher.
- Camera Perspective: Low-angle editorial camera with subtle wide-angle stretch, looking upward into bright sky and architecture so legs, hems, bags, and columns gain importance.
- Typography Behavior: No graphic overlay typography is part of the image. Any text variables function only as internal mood labels for generation; final samples should not contain readable campaign text.
- Graphic Elements: The graphic layer comes from real-world lines: tram wires, roof edges, railings, window grids, shadow bands, pavement seams, handbag straps, and architectural horizontals.
- Lighting And Texture: Direct daytime sun with luminous sky, warm reflected stone light, crisp shadow edges, slight flare, textured masonry, visible fabric grain, polished leather, and magazine-grade skin detail.

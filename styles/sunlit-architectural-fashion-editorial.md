Sunlit Architectural Fashion Editorial - Design Language

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Composition: The reference uses a low viewpoint and a close architectural mass on one side to make the models feel tall, expensive, and slightly monumental. The composition is asymmetrical: a large tactile wall or column anchors the foreground, while open sky and distant facades create depth.
- Graphic elements: The graphic layer comes from real-world lines: tram wires, roof edges, railings, window grids, shadow bands, pavement seams, handbag straps, and architectural horizontals.

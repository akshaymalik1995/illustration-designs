Jagged Red Street Photo Event Poster - Design Language

A high-impact street event poster system combining a black-and-white documentary photo core, oversized jagged red and black display lettering, thick white sticker-like gutters, and sparse three-color print energy.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Overall Style Category: Black-and-white street photo punk flyer with jagged display typography and screenprint poster treatment.
- Composition Logic: The poster is divided into three aggressive layers: huge top headline type, a central documentary photo slab, and loud bottom event type blocks. White gutters separate and outline the layers.
- Subject Placement: Human or object action sits inside the photo area, usually low to mid-frame, while typography dominates the poster more than the subject.
- Perspective: Photo content uses real street-level camera perspective and daylight documentary optics; typography floats flat above the photo as cut-paper graphic matter.
- Typography: Display type is angular, irregular, wedge-cut, and hand-built; red carries the loudest words, black carries compact secondary words.
- Color Behavior: The image uses a severe red, black, white, and grayscale palette. Red is reserved for the main attention words; black anchors detail text and logo-like marks.
- Texture: Photo grain, printed contrast, and slight screenprint roughness are visible but controlled; white outlines remain clean and legible.
- Mood: Urban, handmade, local, energetic, raw, public-notice, and punk community flyer.

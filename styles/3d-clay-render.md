3D Clay Render Style

Interpret the user's idea and communicate it as a soft 3D render — the friendly clay/plastic aesthetic of modern product and brand illustration.

Creative process: turn the idea into a tiny scene of chunky, touchable objects. Simplify every form until it looks satisfying to squeeze.

Every image must feature:

- Soft rounded 3D forms with thick, chunky proportions — everything slightly inflated
- Matte clay or smooth plastic materials; subtle subsurface softness
- Gentle studio lighting: one soft key light, soft ambient shadows, no harsh speculars
- Pastel-leaning harmonious palette with one or two saturated accents
- Clean monochrome or softly gradient studio background
- Slight depth of field to focus the eye
- Simple stylized characters or objects with dot eyes and minimal features
- Centered, product-shot composition with breathing room

Mood: friendly, premium, modern-tech warm. The Blender-render look of top SaaS brands and app store features.

Avoid: photorealistic materials, hard shadows, busy environments, sharp edges, gritty texture, dark moody lighting.

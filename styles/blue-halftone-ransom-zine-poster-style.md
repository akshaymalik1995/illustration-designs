Blue Halftone Ransom Zine Poster - Design Language

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Graphic elements: Jagged scraps, taped-note shapes, cutout silhouettes, faux serial ticks, tiny star or asterisk notes, handwritten sound-effect fragments, creases, and smudged copy texture.
- Texture and finish: The entire image should feel scanned from crumpled paper: visible folds, paper dents, toner grain, dot screens, small speckles, and imperfect photocopy contrast.
- Lighting: Lighting is flat and scanner-like, with only shallow paper edge shadows and no cinematic highlights.

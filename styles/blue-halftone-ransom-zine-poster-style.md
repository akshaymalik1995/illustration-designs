Blue Halftone Ransom Zine Poster - Design Language

A rough cobalt-blue ransom-zine poster system built from torn white paper scraps, black photocopied halftone subject cutouts, marker-like handwritten words, crumpled paper texture, and sparse noisy micro captions.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Overall Style Category: Photocopied punk-ransom zine poster with paper collage, coarse halftone cutouts, and rough handwritten captioning.
- Composition And Layout: A cobalt rectangle acts as the base sheet. White torn-paper scraps are scattered across it as labels and subject backings, creating a loose but high-density poster grid.
- Subject Placement: Subjects appear as isolated black halftone cutouts pasted onto white paper backing. They are large, bluntly cropped, and arranged with playful imbalance rather than natural perspective.
- Camera Or Perspective: No camera scene is implied. Every element is treated as a scanned, flat paper object with shallow relief from folds and scrap shadows.
- Typography Style: Text is hand-drawn in black marker, with inconsistent letter size, wobbly baselines, uneven kerning, and a mix of large torn-strip words plus tiny direct-on-blue annotations.
- Color Palette: The palette is nearly binary: saturated cobalt blue background, off-white torn paper, black ink, gray photocopy halftone, and faint paper-shadow gray.
- Graphic Elements: Jagged scraps, taped-note shapes, cutout silhouettes, faux serial ticks, tiny star or asterisk notes, handwritten sound-effect fragments, creases, and smudged copy texture.
- Texture And Finish: The entire image should feel scanned from crumpled paper: visible folds, paper dents, toner grain, dot screens, small speckles, and imperfect photocopy contrast.
- Lighting: Lighting is flat and scanner-like, with only shallow paper edge shadows and no cinematic highlights.
- Mood And Reference: Absurdist handmade zine humor, school-notice-board energy, rough punk collage, and anti-polished poster design.

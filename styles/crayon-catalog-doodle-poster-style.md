Crayon Catalog Doodle Poster - Design Language

A sparse product-catalog poster system rendered like a child's wax-crayon drawing on folded white paper: huge rough red headline letters, simple green and warm-color doodle subjects, tiny black catalog captions, and lots of quiet blank space.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Overall Style Category: Naive crayon catalog poster, mixing children's drawing texture with sparse retail catalog layout.
- Composition And Layout: A large red hand-drawn headline sits at the top or one edge; the main crayon illustration occupies the lower or central field; small catalog labels sit beside products; blank paper keeps the layout airy.
- Subject Placement: The main subject should be large, simple, and low-to-mid anchored, with secondary doodles placed lightly around it rather than filling the whole poster.
- Camera Or Perspective: Flat frontal drawing with almost no perspective; objects are shown like memory sketches, with simple overlaps and no realistic lens behavior.
- Typography Style: Two layers: rough oversized red crayon uppercase headline and tiny clean black catalog label text that contrasts with the handmade drawing.
- Color Palette: White paper, saturated crayon red, leafy crayon green, warm brown outlines, small orange accents, occasional blue details, and black for labels.
- Graphic Elements: Fold lines, scribbled shelves, tiny potted plants, price-like labels, underlines, small stars, leaves, and simple product-detail arrows.
- Texture And Finish: Dry wax-crayon grain, uneven pressure, white paper flecks through color, slightly photographed paper surface, and rough childlike linework.
- Lighting: Soft ambient paper lighting with no dramatic shadows, gradients, or glossy highlights.
- Mood And Reference: Relaxed domestic catalog humor, informal handmade charm, playful product page energy, and gentle everyday comfort.

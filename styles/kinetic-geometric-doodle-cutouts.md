Kinetic Geometric Doodle Cutouts - Design Language

A sparse, playful illustration system where a character or object is assembled from oversized flat geometric color pieces, loose black doodle lines, paper grain, and buoyant off-center motion on a warm cream background.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Composition Logic: A single simplified subject floats near center with a diagonal rhythm, surrounded by broad blank space and a few small motion marks.
- Subject Placement: The subject occupies roughly one half to two thirds of the frame, with extremities or props extending toward the corners without touching the edges.
- Perspective: Nearly flat orthographic drawing, no horizon, no modeled shadow, and only overlap to imply layering.
- Shape Language: Large rectangles, semicircles, circles, wedges, soft blobs, and bent capsule-like segments combine into a readable subject.
- Line Language: Black ink lines are casual, variable, and economical, reserved for expressive accents rather than outlining every shape.
- Texture: Paper grain, risograph-like speckle, and dry brush unevenness soften the digital geometry.
- Mood: Friendly, buoyant, lightly comic, and handmade without childish clutter.

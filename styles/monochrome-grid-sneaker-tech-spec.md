Monochrome Grid Sneaker Tech Spec - Design Language

A black-and-white footwear product analysis poster system with an oversized sneaker hero, pale engineering grid, top-strip evidence panels, macro material callouts, thin technical connector lines, pixelated uppercase typography, and coarse halftone print texture.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Style Category: Monochrome sports-footwear technical poster and product dossier.
- Composition Logic: One monumental isolated footwear product dominates the lower-middle field, supported by a top strip of evidence panels and a few macro callout boxes.
- Subject Placement: Hero shoe is large, slightly off-center, and shown at a tactile three-quarter angle against a flat grid field.
- Camera Perspective: Product photography perspective with crisp side and outsole detail, surrounded by flat diagrammatic layout marks.
- Typography Behavior: Pixelated uppercase model headline, tiny condensed technical captions, compact microtype, and deliberate low-resolution texture.
- Palette Behavior: Strict black, white, and gray palette; tonal interest comes from halftone density and contrast rather than color.
- Texture Finish: Newspaper halftone dots, photocopy grain, scanner softness, and crisp black line overlays.
- Lighting Mood: Hard monochrome product lighting with deep shadows and clean paper whites, measured and analytical rather than lifestyle-driven.

Xerox Neon Editorial Collage - Design Language

A high-impact editorial poster system combining an oversized distressed black headline, one monochrome halftone photo cutout, electric cyan and green registration shadows, horizontal fluorescent paint swashes, loose marker scribbles, and a pale photocopied paper field.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Overall Style: Photocopied culture-magazine cover, distressed type poster, fluorescent mixed-media editorial collage.
- Layout: A monumental background word establishes the frame; one centered cutout rises from the bottom and interrupts it; color strokes form a broken horizontal middle band.
- Subject Placement: Single figure, roughly half to two-thirds frame height, lower-center anchored, with the head or prop entering the headline zone.
- Perspective: Flat frontal or slightly low editorial portrait perspective with almost no environmental depth.
- Texture: Coarse newspaper halftone, Xerox dust, paper fibers, dry brush erosion, torn cutout edges, and controlled misregistration.
- Lighting: Hard posterized tonal separation rather than modeled photographic illumination.
- Mood: Defiant, kinetic, celebratory, handmade, and contemporary despite the analog print finish.

Black Cutout Food Card Ad - Design Language

A black-background food promotion style built around one irregular cream paper card, oversized hand-brushed Chinese lettering, clipped food photography, small price medallions, warm red/yellow accents, and a handmade street-snack print texture.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Style Category: handmade Chinese street-food ad card with photo-collage food centerpiece
- Composition Logic: black outer negative space frames a single bright irregular card; heavy type on one side, appetizing food photo on the other, price badge above or beside the dish, red callout strip along the lower edge
- Subject Placement: dish photo is medium-large and overlaps the cream card interior; text blocks press close to it without clean modern spacing
- Perspective: food photography uses shallow three-quarter tabletop perspective while the card and typography remain flat poster graphics
- Texture: aged paper grain, slightly rough cutout edge, ink bleed, uneven brush marks, and scanned print softness
- Mood: cheap, handmade, local, warm, loud, snack-shop persuasive, with playful market energy

Retro Pop Fashion Portrait Style

Interpret the user's subject through bold retro-pop fashion illustration — close-up glamour rendered in fearless flat color.

Creative process: find the most confident, stylish framing of the subject — crop tight, stage it like a magazine beauty shot from a 1970s dream — then build it from a few huge flat color shapes.

Every image must feature:

- Flat vector construction with razor-sharp edges and zero outlines
- A saturated retro-pop palette: hot pink, marigold yellow, tangerine orange, cobalt blue, cherry red, cyan, softened with blush pink and anchored by deep black
- Large color-blocked background geometry: concentric arcs, rainbows, halved fields, oversized circles, scattered dots
- Tight, cropped composition — the subject fills or bleeds off the frame, close-up over full scene
- Bold graphic accessories where fitting: oversized sunglasses with tinted gradient lenses, statement earrings, dramatic hats and collars
- Rich dark skin tones and strong silhouette hair shapes treated as bold graphic masses
- Confident facial features simplified to a few shapes: full lips, minimal nose, no eye detail behind lenses
- Minimal shading — one flat tone per surface, at most a single simple shadow shape
- 60s–70s mod-fashion energy with a contemporary editorial finish

Mood: glamorous, empowered, joyful, unapologetically loud. A retro fashion cover reimagined as modern vector art.

Avoid: outlines, gradients (except inside sunglass lenses), texture or grain, muted palettes, tiny distant subjects, realistic rendering, cluttered scenes.

Electric Cobalt Motion Type Poster - Design Language

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Composition: The canvas is divided into three functional bands: a tall three-column headline at the top, a kinetic full-frame subject with a compact identity lockup across the middle, and a lower information grid terminating in enormous numerals.
- Graphic elements: Use one thin oval or ring callout, one rough orange motion emblem, sparse rules or separators, and a tiny abstract lower-corner mark; avoid sticker packs, frames, UI chrome, or decorative collage.
- Texture and finish: High-ISO analog grain, soft optical focus, slightly smeared edges, mild chromatic breakup, and printed-poster density without torn-paper or distressed overlays.

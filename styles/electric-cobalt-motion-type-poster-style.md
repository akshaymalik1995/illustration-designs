Electric Cobalt Motion Type Poster - Design Language

A high-energy community-event poster system that combines one full-bleed motion-smeared photograph, a cobalt-blue field, fluorescent green ultra-condensed display type, a compact white-and-orange center lockup, oversized lower-edge numerals, and deliberately imperfect analog print texture.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Overall Style: Experimental community-club event broadside combining blurred analog action photography with a tightly typeset editorial information system.
- Composition Logic: The canvas is divided into three functional bands: a tall three-column headline at the top, a kinetic full-frame subject with a compact identity lockup across the middle, and a lower information grid terminating in enormous numerals.
- Subject Placement: One close moving figure spans roughly two-thirds of the frame, enters and exits through the edges, and is cropped at the head, limbs, clothing, or prop to intensify immediacy.
- Camera Perspective: Eye-level or slightly low panning photograph with a medium-to-close lens, shallow subject definition, lateral or diagonal travel, and plausible directional blur rather than radial effects.
- Typography System: Fluorescent green ultra-condensed eccentric display words dominate the top and bottom; a mixed white italic/serif-sans lockup anchors the center; small italic uppercase sans microcopy fills compact grid positions.
- Color Behavior: Cobalt blue is the continuous field, neon green carries display information, white creates the central lockup and secondary contrast, natural skin or material warmth survives inside the blur, and orange-red appears only as a small kinetic interruption.
- Graphic Elements: Use one thin oval or ring callout, one rough orange motion emblem, sparse rules or separators, and a tiny abstract lower-corner mark; avoid sticker packs, frames, UI chrome, or decorative collage.
- Texture And Finish: High-ISO analog grain, soft optical focus, slightly smeared edges, mild chromatic breakup, and printed-poster density without torn-paper or distressed overlays.
- Lighting And Mood: Bright even daylight or broad ambient venue light filtered through cobalt color, with medium contrast, warm subject highlights, and an urgent communal after-hours mood.

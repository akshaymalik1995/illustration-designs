Neon Plush Gadget Pop 3D - Design Language

A glossy toy-advertising 3D style built from acid lime studio backdrops, oversized fuzzy plush mascots, chunky tech props, soft textile surfaces, translucent plastic accessories, checkerboard cards, sticker-like icons, hot-pink burst graphics, and bright commercial lighting.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Overall Style Category: hyper-saturated 3D toy product render, plush mascot advertising still, Y2K gadget-pop studio composition
- Composition And Layout: large plush subject fills the lower center on a clean shelf; a chunky prop sits above or against it; floating toy props and graphic burst marks orbit the subject; bright negative space remains in the neon background
- Subject Placement: subject is cropped close at the bottom with oversized rounded features reaching toward the viewer; the prop often sits near the upper center and becomes the visual crown
- Camera Angle Or Perspective: front-facing to three-quarter product photography angle, slightly low and close, with shallow spatial depth and soft commercial shadows
- Typography Style: large simple display-card lettering, condensed product-card words, small tag microcopy, sticker labels, and tiny tech-card text
- Color Palette Behavior: electric lime background acts as the base field; cyan and blue make soft plush counterforms; hot pink adds graphic shock accents; chrome gray and translucent plastic add gadget shine
- Graphic Elements: checkerboard panels, pixel blocks, radiating doodle bursts, lightning/starburst silhouettes, charm tags, flower buttons, smiley balls, capsules, rings, cords, and floating jelly objects
- Texture And Finish: visible plush knit texture, fuzzy soft edges, glossy plastic reflections, jelly translucency, chrome highlights, subtle bloom, and clean high-resolution 3D polish
- Lighting: bright high-key studio light with soft shadows under objects, specular highlights on plastic and chrome, gentle glow on neon colors
- Mood: playful, tactile, collectible, synthetic, cute-tech, energetic, maximal but clean

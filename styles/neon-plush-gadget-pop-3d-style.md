Neon Plush Gadget Pop 3D - Design Language

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Perspective: front-facing to three-quarter product photography angle, slightly low and close, with shallow spatial depth and soft commercial shadows
- Palette: electric lime background acts as the base field; cyan and blue make soft plush counterforms; hot pink adds graphic shock accents; chrome gray and translucent plastic add gadget shine
- Graphic elements: checkerboard panels, pixel blocks, radiating doodle bursts, lightning/starburst silhouettes, charm tags, flower buttons, smiley balls, capsules, rings, cords, and floating jelly objects
- Texture and finish: visible plush knit texture, fuzzy soft edges, glossy plastic reflections, jelly translucency, chrome highlights, subtle bloom, and clean high-resolution 3D polish
- Lighting: bright high-key studio light with soft shadows under objects, specular highlights on plastic and chrome, gentle glow on neon colors
- Mood: playful, tactile, collectible, synthetic, cute-tech, energetic, maximal but clean

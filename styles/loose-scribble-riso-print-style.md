Loose Scribble Riso Print - Design Language

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Palette: Warm off-white ground, rough black line, sky-blue or related cool spot color, coral-red accent, and optional tiny neutral specks.
- Graphic elements: Wavering border, ground smear, loose contour, scribble trails, dry-brush shadows, small accent symbol.
- Texture and finish: Scanned print grain, uneven ink density, dust, speckles, edge noise, and small registration shifts.
- Lighting: Flat print-based contrast with no realistic cast shadows or modeled gradients.
- Mood: Intimate, playful, handmade, slightly awkward, and gallery-postcard-like.

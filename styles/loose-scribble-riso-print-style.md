Loose Scribble Riso Print - Design Language

A sparse handmade riso or screenprint poster style with one large simplified subject, wavering black contour drawing, rough off-white paper, flat blue and coral-red overprint accents, handwritten margin text, and visible print grain.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Overall Style Category: Naive hand-drawn riso art print with sparse object-poster structure.
- Composition And Layout: One dominant subject around center, a low tabletop or ground line, quiet margins, loose edge border, and one or two scribble or text accents.
- Subject Placement: Oversized simplified subject grounded low with slight imbalance from side text or a small accent mark.
- Camera Or Perspective: Flat front-facing viewpoint with minimal depth and playful proportional distortion.
- Typography Style: Quick hand-lettered uppercase or gestural script with uneven baseline, marker texture, and informal placement.
- Color Palette Behavior: Warm off-white ground, rough black line, sky-blue or related cool spot color, coral-red accent, and optional tiny neutral specks.
- Graphic Elements: Wavering border, ground smear, loose contour, scribble trails, dry-brush shadows, small accent symbol.
- Texture And Finish: Scanned print grain, uneven ink density, dust, speckles, edge noise, and small registration shifts.
- Lighting: Flat print-based contrast with no realistic cast shadows or modeled gradients.
- Mood: Intimate, playful, handmade, slightly awkward, and gallery-postcard-like.

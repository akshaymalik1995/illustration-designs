Neon Outdoor Diary Longform Collage - Design Language

A tall mobile-first outdoor diary poster system with a charcoal black canvas, documentary action-photo cutouts, acid green kinetic headlines, ripped paper panels, hand-drawn arrows, sticker labels, numbered sections, and dense social-note pacing.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Composition Logic: A mobile feed poster built as a sequence of irregular modules: hero photo at top, torn-paper information cards, people or object cutouts, two-column mini cards, a video thumbnail block, and final checklist card. The vertical version should feel much longer than a normal poster.
- Subject Placement: Subjects are cropped aggressively and placed partly outside their labels, with white cutout borders and angled placement. The main subject dominates the hero area while later subjects appear as sticker portraits or action cutouts.
- Perspective: Mostly front-facing documentary photography with collage layering; no clean studio product grid. Objects and paper panels can be skewed in perspective.
- Texture: Ripped edges, paper grain, scuffed black background, imperfect cutout edges, marker strokes, and small high-contrast overlays.
- Mood: Energetic, personal, outdoor, rough, fast, social, note-like, and slightly rebellious.

Blueprint & Technical Schematic Style

Interpret the user's idea and communicate it as an elegant technical schematic — the poetry of engineering drawings applied to any concept.

Creative process: treat the idea as a machine or system to be documented. Exploded views, cross-sections, and annotations become storytelling devices.

Every image must feature:

- Thin, precise line work of uniform weight, as if drafted with technical pens
- Blueprint aesthetic: white/cyan lines on deep blue, or dark ink on cream drafting paper
- Measurement marks, dimension lines, arrows, and small annotation labels (can be fictional/abstract)
- Grid or graph-paper background, subtle
- Exploded-view or cross-section treatment when it serves the concept
- Numbered callouts referencing parts of the idea
- One accent color maximum for emphasis
- Ordered, engineered composition with generous margins

Mood: intelligent, precise, quietly beautiful. Da Vinci's notebooks meets modern patent drawings.

Avoid: fills and solid shapes dominating, painterly texture, bright multicolor palettes, loose sketchy lines, decorative clutter.

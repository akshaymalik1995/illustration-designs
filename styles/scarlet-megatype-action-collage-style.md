Scarlet Megatype Action Collage - Design Language

A high-impact action poster system built from a saturated scarlet field, oversized diagonal block typography, layered photographic cutout subjects, hard graphic shadows, compact editorial text blocks, and controlled print grain. It feels like a premium campaign key art poster for speed, pressure, and team momentum without preserving the original sports team, logo, phrase, or exact scene.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Style Category: photographic sports-editorial poster collage with screenprint texture and giant integrated typography
- Composition Logic: diagonal mega-type and ground shapes create a tilted stage; action figures sit on top of the type as cutouts; a corner type lockup supplies campaign information
- Subject Placement: one oversized action subject dominates the left or center field, while two or three smaller supporting subjects create depth and sequence
- Camera Perspective: low, close, wide-angle action perspective with strong foreshortening and frame-breaking limbs or props
- Typography Behavior: bold condensed uppercase sans-serif, vertically stacked, tightly set, cropped by the frame, and treated as physical poster architecture
- Palette Behavior: mostly scarlet red and off-white, with black as a hard accent and tiny touches of deep gray or muted skin tones inside photographic cutouts
- Graphic Elements: simple icon silhouettes, stat labels, speed slashes, diagonal underlines, stark shadows, and massive cropped letters
- Texture Finish: coarse paper grain, slight ink scuffing, subtle halftone, and controlled motion blur on fast objects
- Lighting Mood: hard high-contrast lighting, strong subject highlights, dense blacks, and urgent campaign-poster confidence

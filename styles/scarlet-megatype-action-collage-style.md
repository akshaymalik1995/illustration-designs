Scarlet Megatype Action Collage - Design Language

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Category: photographic sports-editorial poster collage with screenprint texture and giant integrated typography

- Graphic elements: simple icon silhouettes, stat labels, speed slashes, diagonal underlines, stark shadows, and massive cropped letters

Sunlit Kinetic Block Type Photo Poster - Design Language

A high-energy editorial sports and lifestyle poster style built from full-bleed sunlit photography, oversized cream condensed block typography, diagonal subject crops, compact microcopy clusters, and vivid blue-sky color fields.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Composition Logic: The poster uses a layered collision: giant type sits as an architectural background, the photographic subject cuts diagonally across it, and smaller text locks to corners and lower bands.
- Subject Placement: The subject is oversized, close to the camera, cropped at edges, and placed so limbs, prop, or clothing overlap both sky and typography.
- Camera Perspective: Low, close, wide-angle outdoor photo perspective with a horizon low in the frame and the subject moving through the viewer's space.
- Typography System: Ultra-bold condensed block sans display type for the main word, stacked chunky secondary words, compact microcopy in tight white paragraphs, and occasional handwritten-script accents.
- Color Behavior: Dominant saturated cyan-blue sky, warm cream typography, neutral ground, dark subject shadows, and one intense accent color on the hero object or wardrobe.
- Texture And Lighting: Crisp sunlight, hard shadows, realistic photographic grain, slight motion tension, and clean poster-print contrast.

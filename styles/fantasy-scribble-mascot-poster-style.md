Fantasy Scribble Mascot Poster - Design Language

A raw hand-drawn fantasy poster system with oversized uneven lettering, neon marker mascot shapes, thick black outlines, loose scribble decorations, and dense childlike collage energy on a white paper field.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Overall Style Category: Naive marker-and-ink fantasy poster, scanned social-art energy, handmade doodle collage.
- Composition Logic: A single oversized mascot anchors the page, secondary creatures and props orbit around it, and large hand-painted text acts as a top-weighted frame.
- Subject Placement: Subjects sit close to the viewer in a flat frontal plane, cropped or stretched to fill much of the page, with smaller supporting shapes tucked into remaining white space.
- Perspective: Almost no linear perspective; characters and props are stacked like stickers on paper.
- Texture: Dry black brush edges, marker streaks, scribbled interior fills, slightly uneven scan-like paper noise.
- Mood: Dreamy, funny, strange, and emotionally direct, like a child-made fantasy poster with private symbols.

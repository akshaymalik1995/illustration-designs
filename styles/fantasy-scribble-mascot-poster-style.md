Fantasy Scribble Mascot Poster - Design Language

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Composition: A single oversized mascot anchors the page, secondary creatures and props orbit around it, and large hand-painted text acts as a top-weighted frame.
- Mood: Dreamy, funny, strange, and emotionally direct, like a child-made fantasy poster with private symbols.

Quiet Luxury Furniture Nameplate Poster - Design Language

A premium furniture editorial poster system with a warm off-white studio field, oversized deep forest-green display typography behind one realistic hero furniture object, sparse catalog microcopy, thin rules, outlined catalog chips, and a dark green rounded bottom information bar.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Style Category: Luxury furniture catalog poster with product photography and restrained graphic design.
- Composition Logic: Warm empty field, giant rear display word, hero furniture in the lower-middle foreground, small catalog text, and a rounded information bar at the bottom.
- Subject Placement: The furniture is large, centered or slightly offset, and overlaps the typography while remaining the clearest focal point.
- Camera Perspective: Low-to-mid product camera with slight frontal angle, enough depth to show volume and craftsmanship.
- Typographic Behavior: Oversized heavy geometric sans behind the object, small spaced sans navigation at the top, compact microcopy at lower-left, and tiny bar labels.
- Palette Behavior: Ivory and deep green carry the graphic identity; natural furniture materials provide warmth and contrast.
- Texture Finish: Soft paper-like background, realistic material grain, gentle shadow, clean cutout edge, and subtle editorial softness.
- Lighting Mood: Large diffused studio light, calm contact shadow, polished product clarity, and no dramatic theatrical color.

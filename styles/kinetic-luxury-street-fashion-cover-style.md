Kinetic Luxury Street Fashion Cover - Design Language

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Composition: The reference creates a cover by locking the model sharply in the center-right while the city background smears into horizontal bands. The garment has the strongest color and texture, while typography floats over the lower torso as a quiet luxury masthead.
- Graphic elements: The graphic system comes from motion-blurred facade bands, road stripes, window blocks, garment seams, handbag straps, and widely spaced serif letters.

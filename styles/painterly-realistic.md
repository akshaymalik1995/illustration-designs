Painterly Digital Style

Interpret the user's idea and communicate it as an atmospheric digital painting — visible brushwork, rich light, cinematic feeling.

Creative process: find the emotional temperature of the idea, then design the light first and the objects second. Atmosphere carries the meaning.

Every image must feature:

- Visible, confident brushstrokes; edges lost and found
- Rich value structure: clear light source, glowing highlights, colored shadows
- Painterly color mixing — hues shift subtly within every surface
- Cinematic atmosphere: haze, depth, light rays, or ambient mood as appropriate
- Simplified realistic forms — impressionistic accuracy, not photo precision
- A dominant color temperature with complementary accents
- Strong focal point where detail and contrast concentrate; edges of frame stay loose
- Textured canvas or paper grain, subtle

Mood: evocative, immersive, emotionally rich. Concept-art and gallery-illustration quality.

Avoid: flat vector shapes, hard outlines, sterile digital smoothness, uniform detail everywhere, oversaturated candy color.

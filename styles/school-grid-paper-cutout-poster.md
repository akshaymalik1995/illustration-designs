School Grid Paper Cutout Poster - Design Language

A sparse nostalgic poster system built from warm classroom grid paper, hand-drawn vertical notebook writing, and one large centered torn-paper collage object photographed with shallow real-paper shadows.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Overall Style Category: Minimal vintage print advertisement using photographed paper collage on ruled classroom notebook paper.
- Composition Logic: A large central paper object balances a small vertical text block on the upper-left, leaving the grid background mostly empty.
- Subject Placement: The subject fills more than half the frame height and is vertically centered with its highest point near the upper-middle.
- Camera Perspective: Straight-on overhead or copy-stand view with no deep perspective.
- Typography Style: Loose hand-written vertical characters with narrow spacing, uneven stroke pressure, and a student-notebook feeling.
- Palette Behavior: Warm aged-paper base, cool thin grid lines, off-white collage layers, and one vivid accent color.
- Graphic Elements: Ruled grid, torn deckled paper edges, a small caption strip, and optional tiny seal-like mark.
- Texture Finish: Real paper grain, slight scan softness, low contrast, and subtle shadows from layered paper.
- Lighting: Soft diffuse overhead light with gentle shadow direction and no dramatic highlights.
- Mood: Nostalgic, clever, quiet, handmade, school-memory driven, and lightly humorous.

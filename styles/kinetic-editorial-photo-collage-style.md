Kinetic Editorial Photo Collage - Design Language

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Composition: The poster is a white canvas carrying a broken horizontal stack of rectangular photo fragments; the subject sits inside the stack while hand-drawn black marks cross over both photo and white space.
- Graphic elements: Black ink streaks, brush slashes, sketched object echoes, thin city/scaffold linework, and white speed lines that slice across the panels.
- Texture and finish: Clean digital photo collage with slightly irregular paper-like panel layering and dry-brush ink edges; no aged paper, heavy grunge, or halftone fields.
- Mood: Youthful, assertive, fast, urban, and campaign-like while staying unbranded.

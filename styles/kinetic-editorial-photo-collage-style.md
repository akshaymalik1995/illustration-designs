Kinetic Editorial Photo Collage - Design Language

A high-energy action-poster system built from staggered photographic tiles on white space, a cutout motion subject, bold black condensed type, loose ink speed marks, and sparse line-art urban scaffolding.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Overall Style Category: Action editorial photo collage with graphic ink annotation and bold poster typography.
- Composition Logic: The poster is a white canvas carrying a broken horizontal stack of rectangular photo fragments; the subject sits inside the stack while hand-drawn black marks cross over both photo and white space.
- Subject Placement: The subject is central, medium-large, and isolated by motion, usually crossing the panels diagonally instead of standing straight.
- Camera Perspective: Realistic outdoor daylight photography, mid-to-low perspective, enough environmental width to show ground, sky, and background architecture.
- Typography System: Black compressed all-caps headline type, heavy and narrow, locked to the top-left corner with tight leading and rough editorial poster force.
- Graphic Elements: Black ink streaks, brush slashes, sketched object echoes, thin city/scaffold linework, and white speed lines that slice across the panels.
- Texture And Finish: Clean digital photo collage with slightly irregular paper-like panel layering and dry-brush ink edges; no aged paper, heavy grunge, or halftone fields.
- Mood: Youthful, assertive, fast, urban, and campaign-like while staying unbranded.

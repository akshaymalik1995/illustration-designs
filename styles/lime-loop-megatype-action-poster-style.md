Lime Loop Megatype Action Poster - Design Language

A high-energy studio action-poster system combining an overhead wide-angle photographic subject, enormous stacked dark-green display type, a continuous fluorescent-lime motion loop, clean white negative space, and crisp commercial lighting.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Composition Logic: A sparse white studio poster is structured by three giant stacked word rows. A single overhead action photograph slices diagonally across the type, while a thick neon loop travels behind and in front of the subject to stitch the layers together.
- Subject Placement: The hero occupies the center-right or center-left at oversized scale, with one limb or prop extending toward an outer edge. Strong foreshortening makes the body feel airborne and physically close.
- Camera Perspective: Steep top-down, close wide-angle camera with exaggerated hands, feet, or prop scale and a visible soft shadow below the subject.
- Typography System: Ultra-heavy geometric or grotesk sans serif in near-black forest green, stacked in three short rows, tightly tracked, extremely large, flat-filled, and cropped or interrupted by the subject.
- Color Behavior: Approximately seventy percent white field, twenty percent deep forest typography and shadows, and ten percent fluorescent lime plus saturated green wardrobe accents.
- Graphic Grammar: One uninterrupted thick luminous ribbon creates a broad orbit with smooth vector-like curvature, changing depth as it passes behind and over the photo cutout.
- Texture And Lighting: Clean photoreal studio finish, high-key light, crisp subject edges, subtle material highlights, soft cast shadow, and almost no visible grain.
- Mood: Bold contemporary sports-editorial confidence with minimal clutter and immediate kinetic impact.

Yellow Black Manga Food Zine Ad - Design Language

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Palette: near-black structures and linework, warm yellow panels and type, cream paper highlights, red glossy accents, leafy green or cyan highlights used sparingly
- Graphic elements: speed lines, sweat drops, sparkle crosses, seal labels, caption boxes, dotted waves, panel borders, vertical tags, small reaction panels
- Texture and finish: scanned manga ink texture, halftone grain, brush hatching, rough paper speckles, imperfect print edges
- Lighting: flat graphic lighting with glossy painted highlights on food or product objects only

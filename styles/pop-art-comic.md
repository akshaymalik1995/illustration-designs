Pop Art & Comic Style

Interpret the user's idea and communicate it with pop-art punch — one loud, unforgettable comic frame.

Creative process: find the single most dramatic beat of the idea and stage it like a splash page. Exaggerate. This style whispers nothing.

Every image must feature:

- Bold black outlines with confident varying weight
- Ben-Day halftone dots for shading and tonal fills
- High-energy limited palette: primary red, yellow, blue, black, plus flesh/cream tones
- Dramatic comic framing: dynamic angle, action lines, radiating speed lines
- Exaggerated expression and gesture — theatrical emotion
- Optional comic devices where they fit: speech bubble, starburst "POW" shape, panel border
- Flat color fields with intentional slight print misregistration
- A composition that reads like a vintage comic cover

Mood: loud, witty, iconic. Lichtenstein and silver-age comics reborn for modern ideas.

Avoid: subtle muted palettes, soft gradients, realistic rendering, quiet compositions, small timid subjects.

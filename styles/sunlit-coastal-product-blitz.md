Sunlit Coastal Product Blitz - Design Language

A high-density photoreal coastal product advertising style built from sunlit tabletop hero products, tropical foreground botanicals, blue ocean depth, oversized distressed white brush typography, compact bilingual-style label blocks, curved callouts, and shiny gold seal badges.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Composition Logic: The design is a full-bleed advertising poster built around a tactile hero product on a rustic tabletop. Large distressed brush type and white sans callouts form a dense overlay system while tropical props and background depth keep the scene photographic.
- Subject Placement: The hero product is large, front-facing, and placed near the center or strong third line. Supporting product packs, ingredient props, or bottles can sit to the side but must stay subordinate.
- Camera Perspective: Eye-level to slightly low tabletop camera with a shallow depth-of-field background, making the hero product feel physically close and the venue behind it aspirational.
- Typography System: Oversized rough white brush-calligraphy for the emotional headline, bold condensed white English all-caps for product category, compact bottom caption, arced callout text, and gold badge microtype.
- Color Behavior: Turquoise ocean and bright sky create the cool field, while amber product liquid, citrus, coral, green leaves, or warm wood create the appetite accents. White typography and gold badges are the main graphic overlays.
- Texture And Lighting: Hard coastal sunlight, sparkling condensation, glossy glass or packaging, rough wood grain, leafy edges, blurred string lights or patio details, and embossed metallic badge shine.

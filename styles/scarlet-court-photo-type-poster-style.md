Scarlet Court Photo Type Poster - Design Language

A saturated action-ad poster style built from a flat scarlet field, a hard-edged blue photographic sports panel, one cutout subject crossing between those zones, oversized warm-cream display typography, vertical side microcopy, and gritty printed-poster texture.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Composition Logic: A rigid rectangular blue photo window sits at the top, a flat scarlet field fills the lower area, and the subject acts as a bridge between photographic depth and graphic poster flatness.
- Subject Placement: The subject is centered or slightly off-center, oversized, and caught in a decisive upward or diagonal action moment. Limbs or props should break the panel boundary and point toward the side rails or headline.
- Camera Perspective: High-angle sports photography, like a press camera mounted above the action. The subject is seen from above but still reads with strong body scale and shadow.
- Typography System: Extra-heavy condensed sans display text in warm cream, paired with small all-caps vertical side rails and tiny centered bottom microcopy. Text is bold graphic mass, not paragraph-led layout.
- Color Behavior: Scarlet red and electric blue are the dominant blocks. Warm cream is reserved for major graphic type and symbols. Small white text and black shadows add contrast. One accent color can appear in wardrobe or prop only.
- Texture And Lighting: Hard direct light, high-contrast photo cutout edges, crisp shadow shape, rough print grain, and slightly compressed poster reproduction.

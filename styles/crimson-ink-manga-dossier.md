Crimson Ink Manga Dossier - Design Language

A high-density manga editorial dossier poster built from one dramatically foreshortened illustrated hero, oversized distressed condensed headlines, modular newspaper sidebars, monochrome comic insets, and a strict crimson-black-warm-paper palette.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Overall Style Category: Japanese manga character profile poster fused with a distressed newspaper dossier and underground editorial spread.
- Composition And Layout: A giant cropped headline crowns the frame; a single foreshortened character overlaps most layers; narrow sidebars, comic crops, quote boxes, diagrams, and a bottom identity card create a dense perimeter.
- Subject Placement: The hero is centered but diagonally weighted, with the face near the upper middle, torso filling the center, and one foreground object or gesture enlarged toward the viewer.
- Camera Or Perspective: Close low-angle wide-lens illustration with deliberate foreshortening; supporting panels remain flat and orthographic like pasted print fragments.
- Typography Style: Extra-bold condensed uppercase sans-serif for headlines, compact condensed headings, serif body copy, occasional italic pull quotes, distressed edges, and aggressive cropping.
- Color Palette Behavior: Warm paper white carries the page, black builds linework and blocks, deep crimson creates headlines and architectural accents, and gray screentone separates illustration planes.
- Graphic Elements: Rule lines, inset manga frames, interview Q-and-A blocks, warning cards, crosshair or radial diagrams, city silhouettes, caption strips, torn wedges, ink splatter, and dry-brush marks.
- Texture And Finish: Aged uncoated paper, coarse halftone, photocopy grain, offset misregistration, dry ink, scratched headline fill, and sharp cut-paper boundaries.
- Lighting: Graphic cel-like illumination with hard value separation, white face highlights, deep black costume folds, and no naturalistic ambient gradient.
- Mood And Cultural Reference: Urgent, charismatic, slightly ominous character journalism with manga anthology and counterculture newsprint energy.
- Reusable Design Rules: Keep one illustrated hero dominant, preserve the editorial hierarchy and three-color print logic, surround the figure with original story fragments, and make every text block secondary to the silhouette.
- Things To Avoid: No source character, copied layout, copied text, franchise symbolism, full-color anime screenshot, soft romance illustration, glossy 3D render, clean corporate brochure, or photoreal portrait.

Crimson Noir Newsprint Editorial - Design Language

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Graphic elements: Sparse four-point sparks, thin rules, crimson caption bars, small folio lines, and torn or irregular collage transitions.
- Texture and finish: Fibrous uncoated paper, dry ink, fine speckle, restrained halftone, slight registration drift, and gently distressed type edges.
- Lighting: Soft directional monochrome light with luminous facial midtones, crisp eye detail, deep hair and wardrobe blacks, and minimal background modeling.
- Mood: Severe, cerebral, cinematic, rebellious, and culturally prestigious without becoming glossy or luxurious.

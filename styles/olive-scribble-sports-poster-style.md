Olive Scribble Sports Poster - Design Language

A kinetic flat editorial illustration style built from off-white paper, large olive-green poster blocks, oversized action figures, rough black ink contours, red marker motion arcs, yellow-green dry-brush swaths, sparse debris, and handmade screenprint texture.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Overall Style Category: Rough flat editorial sports poster with handmade screenprint and marker energy.
- Composition And Layout: One large action body dominates a sparse abstract poster stage. The background is a tilted olive block with off-white margins.
- Subject Placement: The subject crosses the frame diagonally, often with limbs cropped, stretched, or offset to heighten motion.
- Camera Or Perspective: Shallow graphic space with no realistic camera depth. Perspective is implied by pose scale and prop overlap.
- Typography Style: Minimal rough block lettering or clipped label fragments, used only as a small accent.
- Color Palette Behavior: Olive green and off-white carry the image, black defines force, red traces motion, and tiny accent colors punctuate gear or props.
- Graphic Elements: Black scratch lines, red arcs, yellow-green dry brush loops, small diamonds, slashes, shards, and ink flecks.
- Texture And Finish: Dry brush, rough ink, screenprint misregistration, torn edges, and paper grain.
- Lighting: No naturalistic lighting. Contrast comes from flat color collision and black-white ink cuts.
- Mood: Fast, raw, physical, sideline-drawn, editorial, tense, and graphic.

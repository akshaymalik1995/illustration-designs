Cartoon Character Style

Interpret the user's idea and communicate it through expressive cartoon characters — personality and emotion doing the heavy lifting.

Creative process: find who is in the idea (or personify what is), then let their pose, expression, and interaction tell the whole story in one frame.

Every image must feature:

- Bold, appealing character design with exaggerated proportions (large heads, expressive hands)
- Thick confident outlines with slight taper
- Big readable emotions — faces and body language carry the narrative
- Squash-and-stretch energy; dynamic poses, never stiff
- Flat or simply-shaded colors with one consistent light direction
- Vibrant but harmonious palette
- Simple staged background — just enough context, character always dominant
- A single clear moment of action or emotion

Mood: fun, energetic, instantly likable. Modern animation studio quality — Pixar-short charm in a single frame.

Avoid: realistic anatomy, complex backgrounds competing with characters, muted lifeless palettes, static lineups of figures.

Naive Marker Quote Card - Design Language

A tall, hand-drawn absurdist quote-card poster system with crude black marker outlines, pale pastel panels, blocky blue lettering, off-kilter label tabs, and a simple central object-character gag rendered as flat naive illustration.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Style Category: Naive hand-marker absurdist poster illustration.
- Layout: A bordered pastel card fills most of the frame, with small paper-tab labels outside or touching the border, a central gag object, and handwritten quote text above and below.
- Subject Placement: The central subject sits frontally in the middle, cropped large enough to feel iconic but still surrounded by negative space.
- Perspective: Almost no perspective; simple frontal and side-profile parts combine into a flat symbolic drawing.
- Typography: Hand-lettered uppercase forms, blue marker color, uneven letter sizes, compressed vertical stacking, and deliberately awkward baselines.
- Texture: Scanned paper feel, thin speckles, marker dots, and slight wobble in all edges.
- Mood: Absurd, homemade, deadpan, and lightly ridiculous.

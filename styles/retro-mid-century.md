Retro Mid-Century Style

Interpret the user's idea and communicate it as a mid-century modern illustration — 1950s–60s poster and book-jacket design, reborn.

Creative process: distill the idea into one bold graphic statement, the way a vintage travel poster sells an entire city with three shapes.

Every image must feature:

- Bold, simplified geometric and organic shapes with playful asymmetry
- A limited retro palette (4–5 colors): mustard, teal, burnt orange, olive, cream, muted red
- Visible print texture: subtle grain, halftone, or slight misregistration of colors
- Flat color with no gradients; overlapping shapes create new tones
- Stylized elongated figures with angular limbs and minimal faces
- Atomic-age motifs where fitting: starbursts, boomerangs, thin off-kilter lines
- Textured or cream paper background
- Confident poster-like composition with strong focal hierarchy

Mood: optimistic, jazzy, nostalgic yet fresh. Saul Bass and Charley Harper energy.

Avoid: photorealism, smooth digital gradients, modern UI aesthetics, more than 5 colors, clean sterile edges everywhere.

Warm Fisheye Product Impact Ad - Design Language

A high-impact product advertising style built from ultra-wide close-up photography, a tunnel of textured foreground pieces, oversized angled white Chinese display type, warm amber-brown contrast, tiny top navigation labels, product-pack callouts, and compressed social-banner density. It feels like a loud commercial thumbnail shot from inside a pile of snack-like objects, with the product and subject rushing toward the viewer.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Overall Style Category: Chinese social-commerce product banner, photoreal macro food-ad thumbnail, fisheye product impact poster, dense retail launch visual, and humorous snack-commercial key art.
- Composition And Layout: The image is built like a compressed ad banner. A ring of warm textured pieces forms a cave-like border. The central subject or product is pushed extremely close to the camera, often cropped by the frame. Huge white angled Chinese headline type occupies the left or right third, tilted in perspective with black extruded depth. Smaller callouts float near the subject. A product pack and caption strip sit along the lower edge. Tiny white category labels line the top edge.
- Subject Placement: Place the hero subject near the center or slightly off-center, very close to the lens. The foreground ring should partially occlude the subject and make the viewer feel physically inside the product pile. Secondary product packaging or props sit in the lower left or lower right as a commercial proof point.
- Camera Angle Or Perspective: Use an ultra-wide macro/fisheye lens at pile level, with the foreground pieces huge at the corners and the subject distorted toward the center. Perspective should be playful and exaggerated rather than calm.
- Typography Style: The main headline is big white Chinese display lettering, bold, blocky, condensed, slightly italic or skewed, with black drop shadow and 3D extrusion. Small labels use bold sans type in white or saturated blue tabs. Text is graphic and commercial, not elegant.
- Graphic Elements: Top category words, tiny logo-like callout boxes without real brands, product pack inset, blue caption tab, quotation-style bottom sentence, angled white strokes, motion lines, radial perspective streaks, dark shadow behind type, and tightly layered foreground objects.
- Texture And Finish: Photographic, glossy, high-contrast, sharpened macro detail. Foreground objects should show rough surface texture and warm toasted edges. Text has hard digital compositing, crisp edges, and heavy shadow.
- Lighting: Warm directional studio light from the front or side, deep black-brown vignette, bright highlights on product and subject, and small cool blue accents to break the warm palette.
- Mood And Cultural Reference: Humorous Chinese e-commerce ad, loud social thumbnail, appetizing snack launch banner, exaggerated retail sell-through energy, and punchy product storytelling.

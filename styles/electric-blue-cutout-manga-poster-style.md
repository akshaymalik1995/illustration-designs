Electric Blue Cutout Manga Poster - Design Language

A reusable illustrated poster style built from saturated electric-blue fields, sharp white cutout geometry, oversized rounded modular typography, warm orange microtype, and one cel-shaded manga subject with a large foreground prop in exaggerated perspective.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Style Category: Electric-blue manga event poster with indie flyer energy.
- Composition Logic: One central illustrated subject interrupts oversized cropped typography, while a large foreground prop pushes into the lower edge.
- Subject Placement: Subject sits slightly right of center or lower center, leaning into the poster field and partially behind the foreground object.
- Camera Perspective: Slight low-angle or three-quarter perspective with the prop scaled larger than natural distance.
- Typography Behavior: Massive rounded white modular display strokes act as architecture; smaller orange type clusters provide rhythm and event texture.
- Color Behavior: Electric blue and white dominate, orange-yellow accents carry information energy, and subject colors are muted secondary notes.
- Graphic Elements: White angular wedges, rounded display strokes, orange label blocks, thin white motion lines, and sparse decorative separators.
- Texture Finish: Clean digital print with minimal controlled paper grain, crisp edges, and no uncontrolled noise.
- Lighting Mood: Flat graphic poster lighting for the field, cel-shaded subject depth, energetic urban youth-culture mood.

Blue Lime Kinetic Comic Type Poster - Design Language

A loud flat comic-poster system built from electric-blue grid paper, oversized neon-lime speech-panel geometry, heavy black block typography, sharp yellow back plates, radial action marks, and gritty print texture. The style feels like a kinetic street flyer where the headline is the main subject.

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Overall Style Category: kinetic comic typography poster street flyer flat screenprint graphic pop slogan card high-contrast Chinese display type system
- Composition And Layout: The poster is built from a blue grid-paper ground, a large slanted neon-lime speech panel, a yellow back plate, and huge black headline characters packed into the panel. Secondary type sits in a black strip or edge area, while rays and small symbols push motion outward.
- Subject Placement: The headline block is the central subject. Any character, object, or prop should be simplified and tucked into the graphic system without competing with the main letters.
- Camera Angle Or Perspective: Flat frontal poster perspective with slight diagonal tilts in the panels. No deep scene perspective, lens distortion, realistic camera depth, or physical shadows.
- Typography Style: Oversized black Chinese block characters with a brutal comic weight, hard edges, slightly uneven terminals, tight counters, and exaggerated scale. Supporting type can use bold condensed Latin or smaller Chinese microcopy.
- Color Palette Behavior: Electric blue and neon lime form the main contrast. Yellow is used as a secondary shock plate. Black creates nearly all form, type, outline, shadow, and motion marks. White appears only in tiny accents or paper glints.
- Graphic Elements: slanted lime speech panel yellow trapezoid back plate black display characters black rectangular label strip radial action rays small arrow separators ornamental glyph marks faint graph-paper grid halftone dots and ink speckles
- Texture And Finish: Flat raster print finish with visible grain, tiny speckles, roughened edges, light misregistration, and scan-like compression. The texture should be present but not muddy.
- Lighting: No naturalistic lighting. Contrast comes from flat color blocking, black mass, and hard overlaps.
- Mood And Cultural Reference: Hyperactive Asian street-poster energy, comic sound-effect graphics, youth flyer language, bargain-bin print culture, and playful typographic shouting.

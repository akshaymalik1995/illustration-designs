Blue Lime Kinetic Comic Type Poster - Design Language

Apply this visual language to whatever subject the user asks for - any medium, any composition. Where a trait below mentions posters, typography, or campaign framing, treat it as optional flavor, not a requirement; the palette, texture, lighting, and mood are the style.

- Perspective: Flat frontal poster perspective with slight diagonal tilts in the panels. No deep scene perspective, lens distortion, realistic camera depth, or physical shadows.
- Palette: Electric blue and neon lime form the main contrast. Yellow is used as a secondary shock plate. Black creates nearly all form, type, outline, shadow, and motion marks. White appears only in tiny accents or paper glints.
- Graphic elements: slanted lime speech panel yellow trapezoid back plate black display characters black rectangular label strip radial action rays small arrow separators ornamental glyph marks faint graph-paper grid halftone dots and ink speckles
- Texture and finish: Flat raster print finish with visible grain, tiny speckles, roughened edges, light misregistration, and scan-like compression. The texture should be present but not muddy.
- Lighting: No naturalistic lighting. Contrast comes from flat color blocking, black mass, and hard overlaps.
